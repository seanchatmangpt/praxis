\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This is not the future that was advertised. There is no chrome and there
are no street samurai; the rain on Titan is methane and nobody stands in
it for atmosphere. What there is, in 2030, is a working market: some
tens of thousands of software estates --- terrestrial enterprises, orbital
logistics operators, the Luna fabrication co-operatives, the Mars survey
authorities, and a scatter of long-latency outer-system stations --- trading
not code but \emph{admitted projections}: packs, deltas, and the receipt
chains that make them checkable by strangers. The market is
$n$-dimensional in the plain combinatorial sense: every listing is a point
in a product of closed dimensions (domain, jurisdiction, evidence regime,
latency class, hardware profile, liability tier), and every dimension is
closed because the market's one law --- the Chatman Equation,
$A = \mymu(O)$, with its receipts --- only prices what it can recompute.
The thesis of this account is deliberately anti-dramatic: \emph{the
constraint that shaped interplanetary software commerce was not
bandwidth, ideology, or security theatre, but light}. At $3$ to $22$
minutes one-way, review is impossible, conversation is impossible,
supervision is impossible --- and so the only trust artifact that survives
the crossing is one that verifies in local time from content alone.
The Chatman Equation was not adopted off-world because it was futuristic.
It was adopted because at Mars distance it is the only thing that works,
and by 2030 the same physics-forced discipline had quietly become the
terrestrial default too. The future arrived as paperwork that checks
itself. This document explains the mathematics of that paperwork, reports
from the sites where it runs, and accounts honestly for what it has not
solved.
\end{abstract}

\chapter{Against the Advertised Future}

The fictional futures of the last century organised themselves around
spectacle: augmentation, neon, the corporation as cathedral. What actually
scaled was duller and stranger. The decisive artifacts of 2030 commerce
are TOML manifests, canonical N-quads, and BLAKE3 digests; the decisive
institutions are registries and admission gates; the decisive skill is
writing an ontology whose predicates are worth gating. Nobody in this
account jacks in. They run \(\sync\), read the refusal, and fix the graph.

Post-cyberpunk as nonfiction means taking seriously the least glamorous
prediction: that trust, not computation, was the scarce commodity, and
that trust industrialised the way everything industrialises --- by
becoming cheap to verify, standard to package, and boring to use. The
remainder of this document is the mathematics and the field reports of
that industrialisation, from Frankfurt trading floors to the Saturn-system
relay stations, with the same equation running at every site.

\begin{remark}[Terminology]
Throughout, an \emph{estate} is any governed software installation with
its own receipt chain; a \emph{pack} is a portable projection bundle
(ontology, templates, chain head); the \emph{market} \(\Mkt\) is the set
of listed packs with the admission and settlement machinery around them.
No estate in this account is fictionalised in kind; sites are anonymised
per consortium disclosure rules, and the outer-system reports are drawn
from the public chains those stations publish by charter.
\end{remark}

\chapter{Light as the Regulator}

\section{The latency axiom}

\begin{axiom}[No conversation across the crossing]\label{ax:light}
Let $\lat$ be one-way signal latency between counterparties. Any trust
procedure requiring $k$ interactive round trips costs $2k\lat$ before
value transfers. For Earth--Mars, $\lat \in [3, 22]$ minutes; for the
Saturn system, $\lat \approx 80$ minutes. Hence every procedure whose
correctness depends on \emph{dialogue} --- code review, supervision,
clarifying questions, incident calls --- degrades linearly in $\lat$ and
is abandoned in practice beyond cislunar space.
\end{axiom}

\begin{theorem}[Latency forces content-addressed trust]\label{thm:latency}
Under Axiom~\ref{ax:light}, the only admission procedures with
$\lat$-independent cost are those computable from received content alone:
verification of $\Hash$-values, replay of $\del$-streams against chain
heads, and membership tests in locally-held feasible regions $\Feas$.
Consequently a market spanning heterogeneous $\lat$ must denominate trust
in recomputable artifacts, or partition into latency islands.
\end{theorem}

This theorem is the whole geopolitical story of off-world software in one
line. The Mars survey authorities did not adopt receipted projection out
of enthusiasm for terrestrial governance fashion; they adopted it because
a supplier attestation that requires a phone call is, at Mars, a supplier
attestation that requires forty minutes of silence per question. The
receipt chain is the first trust instrument in engineering history whose
cost of verification is invariant in distance. Commerce followed the
invariance.

\section{Autonomy as a corollary, not a rebellion}

Fiction expected off-world settlements to diverge politically. What
happened instead is that they diverged \emph{operationally} --- forced by
$\lat$ to admit changes locally, on local evidence, against locally-held
gates --- while remaining commercially entangled through federation.
Autonomy was not declared; it was a corollary of Theorem~\ref{thm:latency}.
The Luna co-operatives run the same engine, the same closed frontmatter
vocabulary, the same FM-coded refusals as a Frankfurt bank, and this
sameness, not any treaty, is what lets a Luna fabrication pack settle into
a terrestrial estate within one synodic trading window.

\begin{station}[Mars survey authority, 2029]\label{st:mars}
The authority's estates admit approximately $400$ deltas per sol with no
terrestrial participation. Their charter requires the full chain to be
mirrored Earthside at every conjunction window; auditors on Earth verify
months of Martian operation in under an hour of hash recomputation,
$O(|E|)$ in the supply edges, per Theorem~\ref{thm:limit-recall} below.
The authority's own engineers describe the arrangement without romance:
``Earth doesn't trust us. Earth trusts the arithmetic. That's better.''
\end{station}

\chapter{The $n$-Dimensional Marketplace}

\section{Listings as points in a closed product}

\begin{definition}[Market coordinate system]\label{def:market}
The market $\Mkt$ assigns every listing a coordinate in
\[
  X \;=\; D \times J \times E \times L \times H \times B ,
\]
where $D$ is the domain vocabulary (a registered, closed namespace);
$J$ the jurisdiction/regulatory regime; $E$ the evidence regime (which
admission gates the producer's chain proves were applied); $L$ the latency
class the pack is certified for (interactive, cislunar, planetary,
outer-system); $H$ the hardware/determinism profile; and $B$ the liability
tier its insurer underwrites. Every axis is finite and closed; extending
any axis is a governance act priced to the extender, on the 2027 rule that
\emph{the proposer of a dimension pays for its matrix}.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Search is membership, settlement is verification]
Because $X$ is a finite product of closed sets, procurement --- historically
a negotiation --- reduces to two computable steps: (i) membership: does a
listing's coordinate lie in the buyer's feasible sub-box
$\Feas_{\mathrm{buyer}} \subseteq X$? (ii) verification: does the pack's
chain head recompute, and do its evidence-regime claims ($E$-coordinate)
correspond to gates actually present in the chain? Neither step involves
the producer. A Titan-orbit relay station procures terrestrial navigation
packs by exactly this procedure, with no message ever sent to the vendor.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[What ``$n$-dimensional'' does not mean]
The phrase promises exotica; the reality is an index. The market's
dimensionality is the plain statement that six-or-so closed coordinates
suffice to make any listing findable and checkable by any counterparty in
the system, human or procedural. The exotic version --- continuous embedding
spaces, learned similarity, recommendation --- exists at the market's edges
as \emph{discovery}, and is firewalled from \emph{admission}: a model may
suggest a pack; only arithmetic may admit one. The market survived its
first adversarial decade by never confusing the two.
\end{remark}

\section{Deltas as the traded good}

Mature estates rarely buy whole packs; they subscribe to \emph{delta
streams}: sequences $(\del_i, \text{evidence}_i, \rec\text{-link}_i)$
priced per admitted change. The unit economics follow the calculus of the
2026 thesis directly: a delta's price correlates with its computed blast
radius $\mymu'(\del O)$ (the chain-rule bound on what it may touch), and
its insurability with the evidence regime of its admission. Low-sensitivity
deltas settle automatically; high-sensitivity deltas queue for the buyer's
own gates. The result is the quiet inversion fiction never predicted:
\emph{changes} became the commodity, and \emph{stability} became the
service.

\begin{finding}[The outer-system premium]
Listings certified for the outer-system latency class ($L$-coordinate
maximal) command a durable price premium not for performance but for
\emph{trace quiescence}: at $80$ light-minutes, a projection that rewrites
identical bytes (state-fixed but trace-live, the 2026 \texttt{force}
finding) pollutes scarce downlink with observer noise. The two-grades-of-%
idempotence distinction, an academic refinement in 2026, is a line item on
Saturn-system invoices in 2030.
\end{finding}

\chapter{The Mathematics That Holds It Together}

\section{Recalled foundations}

The market machinery is the 2026--2029 stack unchanged in kind: graphs
admit into $\Ont^{*}$ through evidence-gated deltas; $\mymu$ projects;
receipts chain with $\rec(w \cdot e) = \Hash(\rec(w) \| \phi(e))$;
federation binds producer chain heads into consumer receipts.

\begin{theorem}[Audit as a limit, system-wide]\label{thm:limit-recall}
For the interplanetary supply digraph $G$ --- nodes now separated by
light-minutes --- coherence remains the commutativity of the bound-head
diagram over each consumer's in-neighbourhood, verifiable in $O(|E|)$
local hash recomputations. Latency appears nowhere in the bound; that
absence is Theorem~\ref{thm:latency} made operational.
\end{theorem}

\section{Time without simultaneity}

Interplanetary operation sharpened a discipline the 2026 engine adopted
for humbler reasons: \emph{no wall-clock in hashed material}. There is no
shared ``now'' worth the name between Earth and the outer stations;
ordering is causal, carried by the chains themselves. The receipt DAG is,
in practice, the system's clock: an event's time is its position in the
partial order of bound heads, and disputes about sequence are resolved by
exhibiting paths, not by comparing timestamps. What began as a
determinism rule for replay turned out to be the correct relativistic
posture by accident --- or rather, by the non-accident that both problems
are the same problem: never let an unverifiable ambient quantity into the
evidence.

\begin{proposition}[Conjunction-window consistency]
During solar conjunction, Earth--Mars links degrade for days. Estates on
both sides continue admitting locally; on reconnection, the federation
layer faces divergent chain extensions. Because deltas form a group and
receipts a monoid homomorphism, reconciliation is not a merge heuristic
but a computation: the divergent suffixes are replayed against each side's
gates, conflicts surface as named refusals on specific deltas, and the
reconciled head binds both branches. In four conjunctions of recorded
operation, no reconciliation has required human adjudication of
\emph{facts} --- only, occasionally, of \emph{policy}, which is where
humans belong.
\end{proposition}

\section{The residue, off-world}

The intent-residue ratio $\rho$ --- hand-authored over total admitted
bytes --- plateaued terrestrially near $0.1$. The off-world estates run
lower still (the Mars authority reports $\rho \approx 0.04$), not because
Martian engineers write less but because scarcity discipline forces more
of the system into projectable form: when every hand-written line must be
maintained across a $40$-minute question loop, institutions economise on
exactly the artifact that requires conversation. The residue concentrates
where it always concentrates --- evidence schemas, gate policy, the
asserted core of the ontology --- which is to say: in the places where a
settlement decides what kind of place it is.

\chapter{What It Did Not Solve}

Nonfiction owes its failures. Four stand.

\textbf{Ontology politics did not dissolve; they relocated.} Gating
assertion made the graph's asserted core the site of institutional power.
The disputes are now precise --- about named predicates with named owners
--- which is an improvement, but the market has produced no mechanism for
resolving them beyond the oldest one: people, in rooms (or, off-world, in
long document exchanges shaped by $\lat$). The equation governs what
follows from the graph. It is silent on who the graph belongs to.

\textbf{The discovery edge is a standing adversary.} The firewall between
learned discovery and arithmetic admission holds, but pressure on it is
constant, because suggestion shapes what gets verified. A recommender
that never lies but selectively surfaces is a censor the admission
arithmetic cannot see. The 2030 consortium treats discovery-layer
auditing as an open problem; this document concurs and offers nothing
better.

\textbf{Long-latency incident response remains slow where it matters
least often.} Refusals prevent the common failures locally. The rare
failure that requires cross-planet coordination still pays full price in
$\lat$, and no artifact shortens it; the chains only guarantee that when
the humans finally converse, they argue from identical, verified state ---
which converts disasters into slow meetings, an exchange everyone accepts
and nobody enjoys.

\textbf{The residue does not go to zero, anywhere.} Four years and two
planets of measurement put $\rho$'s floor strictly above zero. Whatever
intent is, some of it does not compress into verifiable form. The market
prices around this honestly (liability tiers attach precisely to residue),
but the 2026 hope of a vanishing hand-written core is, on the evidence,
false --- and its falseness is now itself a measured, receipted fact,
which is the only kind of defeat this programme respects.

\chapter{Conclusion: The Future as Paperwork That Checks Itself}

The settlement of the solar system's software economy did not look like
its fictions because its binding constraint was never dramatised: light
does not negotiate, and so trust had to become arithmetic. The Chatman
Equation crossed the crossing because it is the only trust instrument
whose verification cost is invariant in distance, indifferent to
simultaneity, and executable by a counterparty who will never meet you.
Around that invariance, an $n$-dimensional market assembled itself out of
closed coordinates and priced deltas --- unspectacular, auditable, and
strange chiefly in how little it resembles adventure.

The 2026 thesis closed on a frontier: the boundary of the exhausted is the
frontier of the trusted. The 2030 retrospective proved its converse on
Earth. This account extends it outward, and downward into ordinariness:

\begin{center}
\emph{Across three light-hours of inhabited space, the working
definition of trust in 2030 is: that which recomputes.\\
Everything else is conversation --- and conversation does not
survive the crossing.}
\end{center}
