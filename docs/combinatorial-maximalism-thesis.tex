\title{\textbf{Combinatorial Maximalism}\\[0.6em]
\large Exhaustion as Proof in Context-Bounded Software Construction:\\
the Algebra, Geometry, and Calculus of the Verified Projection Engine}
\author{Sean Chatman}
\date{July 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
\emph{Combinatorial maximalism} is the methodological stance that when a system's
behavioural space is finite by construction --- closed vocabularies, enumerated
flags, discrete file states --- correctness claims must be discharged by
\emph{exhaustion over the whole space}, never by sampling, narration, or review.
This thesis formalises the stance as practised in the construction of a
deterministic graph-to-filesystem projection engine governed by the Chatman
Equation $A = \mymu(O)$. We give the configuration space of the write
discipline an exact product structure and prove total coverage of its
$144$ cells against an independent reference model; we organise pack
composition as a simplicial complex whose $\binom{8}{2}=28$ edges and single
top face were verified collision-free; and we develop the delta calculus in
which projection idempotence, replay, and drift acquire the standard shapes of
fixed points, integrals, and metrics. Three empirical findings anchor the
mathematics: an aggregate that failed to be a \emph{function} of its inputs
(the \textsc{group\_concat} incident), a fixed point that held at the level of
states but not of annotations (the \texttt{force} finding), and the
predictive success of the exhaustive matrix, whose one flagged tension was
independently re-encountered as a live defect before the ink was dry. The
central theorem of the thesis is sociotechnical as much as mathematical:
under context-bounded construction, \emph{exhaustion is cheaper than trust},
and every place where the space was not exhausted is exactly where the
defects lived.
\end{abstract}

\chapter{The Maximalist Principle}

\section{The stance}

Let a system expose a behaviour $b : X \to Y$ where the input space $X$ is
\emph{finite by construction}. The combinatorial maximalist asserts:

\begin{axiom}[Exhaustion]\label{ax:exhaustion}
If $|X| < \infty$ and membership in $X$ is decidable, then the only admissible
verification of a specification $\hat b : X \to Y$ is the total check
\[
  \forall x \in X:\quad b(x) = \hat b(x),
\]
executed against the running system, with the witness for every $x$ recorded.
Sampling, review, and reported success are inadmissible whenever exhaustion is
feasible.
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}[Independent reference]\label{ax:reference}
The specification $\hat b$ must be computed by an artifact \emph{other than}
$b$ --- a reference model written from the documented law, not read off the
implementation. Verifying $b$ against itself is the self-certification
failure mode.
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}[Closure precedes exhaustion]\label{ax:closure}
Exhaustion is only possible over spaces that have been deliberately
\emph{closed}: vocabularies that refuse unknown members by name, enumerations
with no wildcard arms, flag sets declared rather than discovered. Closure is
therefore not a stylistic preference but the precondition of proof.
\end{axiom}

Axiom~\ref{ax:closure} explains why the same three engineering laws
(closed vocabulary; status earned by evidence; chained history) precipitated
independently in five sibling systems: they are the minimal conditions under
which Axiom~\ref{ax:exhaustion} can be applied at all.

\section{Why maximalism, not minimalism}

The classical testing doctrine is minimalist: choose representative points,
partition by equivalence classes, argue the rest by analogy. That doctrine
presumes an analyst who holds the whole space in mind while choosing the
representatives. The empirical record of the construction examined here shows
the presumption false: every defect arose at a \emph{context boundary}, where
no single participant --- human or model --- held both halves of an
interaction. When the analyst's memory is the weakest component, the
partition argument inherits its weakness. Exhaustion removes the analyst from
the trusted base.

\begin{remark}
Maximalism is not profligacy. The spaces exhausted below have cardinalities
$144$, $28$, $10$, and $8$: closure keeps them small. The maximalist bet is
that \emph{designing for small closed spaces and then exhausting them} beats
designing for open spaces and then sampling them.
\end{remark}

\chapter{Algebra: the Structure of the Exhausted Spaces}

\section{The write discipline as a product space}

The engine's write stage decides, for each rendered artifact, one of a closed
set of outcomes. Its inputs form a product of finite dimensions:

\begin{definition}[Write configuration space]\label{def:sigma}
\[
  \Sig \;=\; F \times U \times I \times S \times T
\]
with
$F = \{\bot,\top\}$ (\texttt{force}),
$U = \{\bot,\top\}$ (\texttt{unless\_exists}),
$I = \{\bot,\top\}$ (\texttt{inject}),
$S = \{\varnothing, s_{=}, s_{\neq}\}$ (\texttt{skip\_if} absent, matching,
non-matching), and $T$ the target-file state. For $I=\bot$,
$T = \{\mathrm{absent}, \mathrm{identical}, \mathrm{different},
\mathrm{anchored}\}$; for $I=\top$ the anchored state refines into the anchor
modes $\{\mathrm{append}, \mathrm{before}, \mathrm{after},
\mathrm{at}, \mathrm{at}_{\mathrm{oob}}\}$. The realised cardinality is
\[
  |\Sig| \;=\; 2\cdot 2\cdot 3\cdot\bigl(4 + 3 + 5\bigr) \;=\; 144 .
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Decision codomain]
$\Out = \{\mathrm{Written},\ \mathrm{Injected},\
\mathrm{Skip}_r,\ \mathrm{Refuse}_c\}$, where $r$ ranges over named skip
reasons and $c$ over the closed error-code set
$\{\text{FM-WRITE-}002,\dots,005\}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[First-match-wins composition]\label{def:fmw}
The decision function is a \emph{priority composition} of guarded rules
$\rho_1 \triangleright \rho_2 \triangleright \dots \triangleright \rho_k$:
\[
  \wdec(\sigma) \;=\; \rho_{j(\sigma)}(\sigma), \qquad
  j(\sigma) = \min\{\, i : \sigma \in \mathrm{dom}(\rho_i) \,\}.
\]
Priority composition is associative and idempotent but \emph{not}
commutative; the order of the rule list is semantics.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Total coverage of the write matrix]\label{thm:matrix}
Let $\hat\wdec$ be the reference model transcribed from the documented decision
table (escape $\to$ \textnormal{FM-WRITE-002}; \texttt{unless\_exists}
$\to$ skip; \texttt{skip\_if} match $\to$ skip; inject family
$\to$ \textnormal{Injected} or \textnormal{FM-WRITE-003/004}; force
$\to$ \textnormal{Written}; absent $\to$ \textnormal{Written}; identical
$\to$ \textnormal{Skip}; different without force $\to$
\textnormal{FM-WRITE-005}). Then, as executed against the real filesystem,
\[
  \forall \sigma \in \Sig:\quad \wdec(\sigma) = \hat\wdec(\sigma),
\]
with a cardinality guard $\#\{\sigma\ \text{exercised}\} = 144$ preventing
silent shrinkage of the exhausted space.
\end{theorem}

\begin{finding}[Force precedes identity]\label{find:force}
The single tension surfaced by Theorem~\ref{thm:matrix} is not a
counterexample but a precedence fact: because
$\rho_{\mathrm{force}} \triangleright \rho_{\mathrm{identical}}$ in
Definition~\ref{def:fmw}, a configuration with $F=\top$ and
$T=\mathrm{identical}$ yields $\mathrm{Written}$, never
$\mathrm{Skip}_{\mathrm{unchanged}}$. The implementation matches the law;
the law diverges from the na\"ive expectation. Chapter~4 shows this is a
distinction between two \emph{grades} of idempotence.
\end{finding}

\section{The vocabulary group and the refusal boundary}

\begin{definition}[Closed vocabulary]
The frontmatter vocabulary is a finite set
$V = \{\texttt{to}, \texttt{sparql}, \texttt{construct}, \texttt{inject},
\texttt{before}, \texttt{after}, \texttt{at\_line}, \texttt{skip\_if},
\texttt{unless\_exists}, \texttt{force}, \texttt{when},
\texttt{skip\_empty}\}$, $|V| = 12$, together with a refusal map
$\neg V \to \mathrm{Refuse}_{\text{FM-TPL-002}}$ that names the offending key.
\end{definition}

Exhaustion here has two sides: all $12$ members admit; a chosen adversarial
sample of $10$ plausible non-members (\texttt{vars}, \texttt{sh\_before},
\texttt{backup}, \texttt{from}, \texttt{mode}, \dots) refuses by name.
The complement of a closed set is infinite, so Axiom~\ref{ax:exhaustion}
applies only to $V$ itself; the non-member check is a boundary probe, and
honesty requires saying so.

\section{The delta group}

\begin{definition}[Delta group]\label{def:delta}
For canonical graphs $O, O'$ over vocabulary $V$, define
$\del(O,O') = (A, D)$ with $A = O' \setminus O$, $D = O \setminus O'$.
Deltas compose by
\[
  (A_1, D_1) \oplus (A_2, D_2)
  \;=\;
  \bigl( (A_1 \setminus D_2) \cup A_2,\; (D_1 \setminus A_2) \cup D_2 \bigr),
\]
with identity $\varnothing = (\emptyset,\emptyset)$ and inverse
$(A,D)^{-1} = (D,A)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Verified group laws]\label{prop:group}
As property-tested over randomly generated graph pairs:
$\del \oplus \del^{-1} = \varnothing$;\quad
$(\del^{-1})^{-1} = \del$;\quad
$O \oplus \del(O,O') \simeq O'$ (replay identity, checked by hash equality).
\end{proposition}

\section{The hash homomorphism and the incident of the non-function}

\begin{definition}[Canonical hash]
Let $\approx$ be the congruence generated by blank-node relabelling and
insertion order. The state hash is
$\Hash = \mathrm{BLAKE3} \circ \canon : \Ont/\!\approx\ \to \{0,1\}^{256}$,
where $\canon$ sorts canonical N-quads (with a bounded colour-refinement pass
for blank nodes).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Well-definedness, exhaustively probed]
$\Hash$ is constant on $\approx$-classes: verified under (i) permutation of
insertion order and (ii) blank-node renaming, both as property tests over
generated instances.
\end{proposition}

\begin{finding}[The \textsc{group\_concat} incident: an aggregate that was
not a function]\label{find:gc}
The self-hosting layer projected a CLI route's flag list via
$\mathrm{GROUP\_CONCAT}$ over an \emph{unordered} solution multiset. SPARQL
guarantees no aggregate ordering; the projection was therefore a
\emph{relation}, not a function, on the quotient by solution order. The
realised failure was maximally quiet: the two flags were both \texttt{bool},
so the swapped signature
$\mathsf{sync\_run}(\mathtt{watch}, \mathtt{dry\_run})$ type-checked against
the handler's fixed $(\mathtt{dry\_run}, \mathtt{watch})$ and inverted the
semantics of both flags at the CLI. The repair --- an inner
$\mathrm{ORDER\ BY}$ subquery --- is precisely the completion of the diagram
\[
  \text{solutions} \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{sort}\ }
  \text{sequence} \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{concat}\ } \text{string},
\]
restoring functionality by factoring through a canonical total order. Moral,
in the algebraic register: \emph{every projection out of a quotient must be
shown constant on equivalence classes, and where the standard gives you a
multiset, you must pay for the order yourself.}
\end{finding}

\section{Receipt chains as a monoid homomorphism}

\begin{definition}[Chain functional]
Histories form the free monoid $E^{*}$ over receipt payloads. The chain map
$\rec : E^{*} \to \{0,1\}^{256}$ is the left fold
$\rec(\epsilon) = g$ (genesis, the all-zero word) and
$\rec(w\!\cdot\!e) = \mathrm{BLAKE3}\bigl(\rec(w) \,\|\, \phi(e)\bigr)$,
with $\phi$ the canonical payload serialisation.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Tamper evidence, adversarially exhausted]
For each index $i$ of a three-link chain, mutation of any field of $e_i$
changes $\rec$ of every prefix extending $i$; the verifier names the least
broken index. Verified for payload mutation, chain-hash mutation,
truncation, and reordering --- the full (small, closed) set of single-step
tamper moves.
\end{proposition}

\chapter{Geometry: Composition Space and Drift}

\section{The pack complex}

\begin{definition}[Pack composition complex]\label{def:complex}
Let $\Packs = \{p_1,\dots,p_8\}$ be the pack set. For $S \subseteq \Packs$
let $\sync_S$ denote projection of a consumer project admitting exactly $S$.
Say $S$ is \emph{coherent} if $\sync_S$ succeeds with all packs' distinctive
outputs present and no output-path collision. The coherent subsets form a
simplicial complex $K \subseteq 2^{\Packs}$ (coherence is inherited by
subsets, since removing a pack removes only its own contributions).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Verified skeleton and top face]\label{thm:complex}
The following faces of $K$ were verified by execution:
all $8$ vertices (single-pack syncs); all $\binom{8}{2} = 28$ edges
(pairwise syncs, fresh state each); and the top face $\Packs$ itself
(the mega-project), including lockfile enumeration, receipt coverage of all
eight content hashes, and second-sync idempotence.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Why edges suffice between the vertices and the top]
Full exhaustion of $2^{8} = 256$ subsets was not performed, and the omission
is principled: pack interactions in this engine factor through exactly two
channels --- ontology union and output-path union --- and both are
\emph{pairwise-generated}: a collision or prefix clash among $k$ packs
implies one among some pair. Hence coherence of all edges plus the top face
(which exercises the union of all channels simultaneously) covers the lattice.
Where interactions cease to be pairwise-generated --- e.g.\ future
\texttt{construct} rules of one pack feeding \texttt{select}s of another ---
this argument lapses and the maximalist must widen the exhausted skeleton.
The boundary of the method is stated, not hidden.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Order invariance]
The graph-union channel is a commutative idempotent monoid
$(\Ont, \cup, \emptyset)$; consequently declaration order of packs is
immaterial, verified by syncing reversed declarations and obtaining a
byte-identical receipt payload.
\end{proposition}

\section{Drift as distance; admission as membership}

\begin{definition}[Drift metric]
On canonical graphs, $d(O,O') = |O \,\triangle\, O'|$, the symmetric
difference; equivalently $d(O,O') = |A| + |D|$ for
$\del(O,O') = (A,D)$. On artifacts, drift is measured per file by hash
inequality against the receipt's recorded output map.
\end{definition}

The engine's health verbs are radius measurements in three projections of
one state space: lock drift ($d$ between locked and on-disk pack content),
staleness ($d$ between receipted outputs and disk), and orphanhood
(outputs whose preimage under the current template set is empty ---
a point outside the image of $\mymu$). Refusal is the assertion
$d > 0 \Rightarrow$ named error; the corruption sweep verified that
perturbing any single pack moves exactly one coordinate and that the refusal
names it and nothing else.

\begin{definition}[Feasible region]
Admission constraints (SHACL shapes, \texttt{when} guards, closed
vocabularies) carve a feasible region
$\mathcal{F} \subseteq \Ont$; $\mymu$ is a partial map with
$\mathrm{dom}(\mymu) = \mathcal{F}$, and fail-closed means the complement of
$\mathcal{F}$ maps to named refusals rather than to best-effort artifacts.
\end{definition}

\section{Projection as retraction}

\begin{theorem}[Idempotence as fixed point]\label{thm:retract}
For fixed admitted inputs $(O^{*}, C^{*}, P^{*}, T^{*})$, the map
$F \mapsto \sync(F)$ is a retraction of filesystem space onto its image:
$\sync \circ \sync = \sync$, verified at byte level (output files identical)
and at payload level (graph hash, output hashes, pack hashes identical)
across repeated runs and across independently created state
(property-tested over generated projects).
\end{theorem}

Geometrically: the admitted state $F^{*} = \sync(F^{*})$ is the unique fixed
point in each fibre, and every sync is a projection onto it. Drift moves you
off the fibre; the next sync either brings you back (\texttt{force}) or
refuses by name (default) --- but never silently splits the difference.

\chapter{Calculus: Deltas, Replay, and the Two Grades of Idempotence}

\section{Differentiation and integration of history}

\begin{definition}[Discrete derivative and integral]
For an ontology trajectory $O_0, O_1, \dots, O_n$, the derivative is
$\del_i = \del(O_{i-1}, O_i)$ and the integral is replay:
\[
  O_n \;=\; O_0 \oplus \bigoplus_{i=1}^{n} \del_i ,
\]
a discrete fundamental theorem verified term-by-term against the receipt
chain: $\rec$ of the prefix at step $i$ must equal the
$\mathrm{prev}$-pointer of step $i+1$, genesis-rooted.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Chain rule across the pipeline]
Writing $\mymu = \mymu_5 \circ \dots \circ \mymu_1$ (resolve, admit, enrich,
extract, render/write), a perturbation $\del O$ propagates as
$\del A \subseteq \mymu_5'\!\cdot\!\mymu_4'\!\cdots\mymu_1'\,(\del O)$:
each stage's sensitivity bounds which artifacts a graph change may touch.
The enrichment stage $\mymu_3$ is a closure operator
(extensive, monotone, idempotent), so its ``derivative'' at a fixed point is
the identity on already-entailed triples --- the formal content of
``running enrichment twice adds nothing.''
\end{proposition}

\section{The two grades of idempotence}

Finding~\ref{find:force} and its live recurrence (a freshly authored pack
shipped with \texttt{force:\,true} and failed the second-sync gate) force a
distinction the na\"ive statement of Theorem~\ref{thm:retract} blurs.

\begin{definition}[State- and trace-idempotence]
Let $\sync$ act on states $F$ and also emit an annotation trace
$\tau(F) \in \Out^{*}$ (the per-file decisions bound into the receipt).
Call the system \emph{state-idempotent} if $\sync(\sync(F)) = \sync(F)$,
and \emph{trace-idempotent} if additionally
$\tau(\sync(F)) = \tau(\sync(\sync(F)))$ up to the intended
$\mathrm{Written} \to \mathrm{Skip}_{\mathrm{unchanged}}$ transition.
\end{definition}

\begin{finding}[Force is state-idempotent but not trace-quiescent]
With \texttt{force:\,true} the second sync rewrites identical bytes:
the state fixed point holds, the byte comparison holds, but the trace
reports $\mathrm{Written}$ forever and the file's modification time advances
each run. Under a filesystem watcher this non-quiescence is not cosmetic:
it is a self-excitation term, and the engine's watch mode oscillated on
exactly this coupling until the lockfile write was made trace-quiescent
(skip when bytes match). The calculus lesson: \emph{a fixed point of the
state map is insufficient; the closed loop formed with any observer of
side-channels (mtimes, events, logs) requires quiescence of the trace as
well.} Formally, the watch loop computes
$F_{k+1} = \sync(F_k)$ triggered by $\mathrm{events}(F_k \to F_{k+1})$;
convergence demands $\mathrm{events}(F^{*} \to F^{*}) = \emptyset$, a
strictly stronger condition than $F^{*}$ being fixed.
\end{finding}

\section{Sensitivity of the guard cross}

The $2 \times 2 \times 2$ cross of \texttt{when}-guard truth, result-set
emptiness, and \texttt{skip\_empty} was exhausted by execution (eight cells,
each asserting both the decision string and the presence/absence of the file).
In calculus terms this is the verification that the piecewise definition of
$\mymu$ has no unglued seams: on each cell the projection restricts to the
documented branch, and the branches agree on their shared boundaries
(empty result with guard false is skipped for the guard's reason, not the
emptiness's --- precedence again, exhaustively pinned).

\chapter{The Maximalist Theorems}

\begin{theorem}[Exhaustibility]\label{thm:exhaust}
If a subsystem's inputs are confined to a product of closed dimensions
(Axiom~\ref{ax:closure}), then total verification against an independent
reference model (Axiom~\ref{ax:reference}) is decidable in
$O(|\Sig|)$ executions, and $|\Sig|$ is a design variable: closure
decisions made for refusal's sake simultaneously make proof cheap.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Predictive power of exhaustion]\label{thm:predict}
An exhaustive matrix does not merely confirm; it \emph{forecasts}. The one
tension it flagged (Finding~\ref{find:force}) was re-encountered as a live
defect in independently authored code before any consumer existed. A sampled
suite that skipped the $F=\top,\ T=\mathrm{identical}$ cell --- the natural
cell to skip, since ``force obviously overwrites'' --- would have shipped the
oscillation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Locality of the defect set]\label{thm:local}
Every defect in the construction record lay outside some exhausted space at
the moment of its introduction: the aggregate-order relation (no ordering
dimension had been closed), the manifest clobber (no exhausted check compared
disk to intent across sessions), the lockfile self-excitation (the trace
dimension was not yet in the matrix), the pack \texttt{force} recurrence
(pack authors' space excluded the write-precedence dimension). Conversely,
no defect was found \emph{inside} a space after its exhaustion. The
maximalist reading: the boundary of the exhausted region is the support of
the defect distribution.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[The programme]
Verification effort should be spent enlarging the closed, exhausted region
--- adding dimensions (trace quiescence, aggregate order, cross-session disk
state) to $\Sig$ --- rather than deepening scrutiny inside it. Each of the
2028--2030 frontier items (triple admission gating, receipt federation) is
the closure of one presently-open dimension.
\end{corollary}

\chapter{Conclusion}

Combinatorial maximalism is often mistaken for brute force. This thesis has
argued it is the opposite: a \emph{design} discipline in which vocabularies
are closed so that spaces become finite, spaces are given product structure
so that exhaustion is enumerable, and exhaustion is executed against
independent reference models so that no participant's memory --- human or
machine --- remains in the trusted base. The algebra supplied the objects
(priority compositions, delta groups, hash homomorphisms on quotients,
chain monoids); the geometry supplied the shapes (the pack complex, the
drift metric, projection as retraction); the calculus supplied the dynamics
(replay as integration, the chain rule of the pipeline, and the two grades
of idempotence whose gap is precisely where a live system oscillates).

The empirical record closes the argument. Where the space was exhausted, no
defect survived. Where a dimension was still open, a defect was waiting ---
quiet, type-correct, and confidently reported as done. Between those two
observations lies the whole thesis:

\begin{center}
\emph{The boundary of what you have exhausted is the frontier of what you
may trust.}
\end{center}
