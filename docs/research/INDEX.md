# Research Archive Index

This directory serves as the historical and theoretical archive for the Praxis/Mfact project. It contains foundational research, previous drafts, and deep-dive formalisms that inform the current autonomic thesis, but which are not part of the active L1 Explore narrative.

## Contents

- **[old-120-page-thesis/](./old-120-page-thesis/)** 
  The original 120-page highly theoretical manuscript (`combinatorial_maximalism_thesis.pdf`). It contains the L3 Formalization of the DfCM framework, including the category theory, colored operads, and bounded candidate space calculations. It was archived to clear the slate for the L1 Explore "Industrial Manufacture of Knowledge Work" thesis.

- **[recursive_workflow.pdf](./recursive_workflow.pdf)**
  The 23-page formal calculus manuscript. It formalizes standing-preserving substitution, recursive closure, and the Chatman Equation ($A = \mu(O^*)$). It is archived here as L3 Formalization reference material.

- **[mfact-20-page-paper/](./mfact-20-page-paper/)**
  The 20+ page `combinatorial_maximalism` paper previously located in the `mfact` repository. It was the initial draft addressing combinatorial expansion but has been fully retired in favor of the newly established L1 Explore Phase Change thesis methodology.
