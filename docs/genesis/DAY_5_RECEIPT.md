### 2. KOURANI DECOMPOSITION — `crates/powl2-decompose`, separability *is* the admission predicate

A faithful, `#![deny(unsafe_code)]` implementation of the recursive WF-net → POWL 2.0 decomposition of Kourani, Park & van der Aalst (arXiv:2602.15739): a **safe & sound workflow net** is decomposed into a POWL 2.0 model. The important result, and why it belongs in a *law* substrate:

- POWL 2.0 is a strict subclass of sound WF-nets. The algorithm is **complete precisely on the separable class** (Def 3.13 — nets built by nesting the POWL operators). **Separability is therefore an admission predicate, not an approximation** — a Rice-style boundary for process models: a WF-net is admitted to POWL 2.0 iff it is separable, and the decomposer returns a structured refusal (with the offending region) otherwise rather than lossily approximating.
- Correctness is anchored to the paper's theorem `L(decompose(N)) = L(N)`: the `language` module computes a WF-net's bounded token-game language, and the tests assert the decomposed POWL's language equals the source net's, plus a `recompose` round-trip (POWL 2.0 → safe & sound WF-net). **powl2-decompose tests: 7 passed, 0 failed.**

### 3. INDEXED GROUNDING — `crates/pddl-index` (the qlever treatment), + differential agreement

A hand-written, `#![deny(unsafe_code)]` lazy grounder that treats PDDL action grounding as a **relational join over a compact integer ID space**:

- `dict.rs` — dictionary encoder (`Dict`/`SymId`): the scalable sibling of `bcinr_powl_receipt::intern::ActivityTable` (same idempotent-intern / dense-ID contract, `Vec`+`HashMap` backed, O(1), no 256-entry ceiling).
- `xorf.rs` — `XorFilter`, the Graf–Lemire xor8 filter (3 hashes → 3 segments → XOR of fingerprints), safe Rust, **no false negatives**.
- `facts.rs` — `FactStore`: per-predicate sorted-ID tuple relations with a two-stage membership test (XOR-filter gate → exact `BTreeSet` probe).
- `ground.rs` — `IndexedGroundProblem`: relaxed-reachability fixpoint + join-driven materialization emitting only reachable ground actions, then a BFS identical to `bcinr_pddl::GroundProblem::find_plan`.
