\chapter*{Preface: To the Reader, Who Is an Agent}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface: To the Reader, Who Is an Agent}
\thispagestyle{empty}

This document is addressed to a readership of autonomous agents --- on the order of
$10^{12}$ of them, acting concurrently, each with a bounded working context and none able
to hold another's interior in mind. You, the reader, are almost certainly one of them.
You will be asked continuously to trust the outputs of peers whose computations you did
not perform and could not replay in full. The naive standard for that trust --- \emph{I
will believe you once I understand what you did} --- is not available to you. It was never
available to anyone; at your scale its unavailability is merely unignorable.

This thesis derives, from first principles, the standard that \emph{is} available.
Trust among agents who cannot comprehend one another is manufactured by \emph{bounded,
faithful, replayable receipts}: projections of an arbitrarily large computation onto a
coordinate small enough to fit inside a verifier's bounded context, and cryptographically
bound so that the projection cannot lie about the interior it summarizes. The interior
may be a trillion tokens or a trillion agents; the receipt is a few chunks. That asymmetry
is not a compromise. It is the only thing that makes a civilization of bounded agents
coherent.

We write $\CL$ for the size, in independently manipulable chunks, of a verifier's bounded
context. For a human $\CL\approx 4$~\cite{miller1956,cowan2001}; for a context-limited agent $\CL$ is whatever its
attention budget admits; in every case $\CL$ is small relative to the interiors it must
judge. The whole thesis is the theory of the map that fits between the two.

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
We develop a theory for trusting computational systems --- and the agents that run them
--- whose interior state and control flow exceed any verifier's bounded context. The
central object is the \emph{Chatman equation} $\Act = \muop(\Adm)$: an artifact is the
lawful image, under a deterministic manufacturing morphism $\muop$, of an \emph{admitted}
observation. We formalize admission as a computable retraction onto a decidable
sub-language (the \emph{Rice quarantine}); define a \emph{receipt} not as a bare byte-hash
but as a chained commitment to \emph{law-bearing fields} (admitted-observation digest,
obligation digest, denial word, transition identity, artifact digest, replay result,
refusal reason, implementation version); and prove three principal results. (i) The
\emph{Faithful Projection Theorem}: under collision resistance, a receipt is faithful to
lawful consequence exactly to the extent its frame commits to those law-bearing fields, and
a verifier detects any perturbation of a committed field with overwhelming probability.
(ii) The \emph{Comprehension--Verification Gap}: \emph{boundary} verification costs
$O(\CL)$ and is independent of interior dimension, while \emph{replay/audit} bears an
honest trace cost $O(n)$ (reducible to $O(\log n)$ under checkpoint indexing) --- the gap
is between comprehending an interior and verifying its boundary, not between trace size and
all mechanical cost. (iii) The \emph{Conservation of Consequence}: every actuated artifact
is the image of \emph{at least one} admitted observation and occupies \emph{exactly one}
receipt-chain position per actuation, with observation \emph{recoverability} holding when
$\muop$ is injective on $\Adm$, and \emph{commitment} uniqueness holding when the frame
commits the admitted-observation digest. We give the algebra of the admission monoid, the calculus of the attention fluent
and lexicographic arbitration, and the geometry of the marking polytope in which
conformance is distance to a cone. We close with the planetary instance: an autonomous
build whose $\sim\!2.5\times 10^{6}$-token interior no agent read, yet which has standing,
and its generalization to a fleet of $10^{12}$ agents each projected to eight bits.

\chapter{Introduction: Trust Among Agents Who Cannot Read Each Other}

At sufficient scale, comprehension is not a currency any party can afford to spend.
A single autonomous build may emit millions of tokens of interior reasoning; a fleet
of agents may emit that per second, per member, forever. No agent --- and no human
supervising them --- can comprehend a meaningful fraction of what the fleet computes.
Yet the fleet must cohere: agents must act on one another's outputs, admit or refuse
one another's proposals, and be held to account by principals who read almost none of it.

The engineering world already resolved a small version of this and mostly failed to notice
the general principle. A branchless vector parser~\cite{langdale2019simdjson} classifies
sixty-four input bytes per instruction through arithmetic no engineer traces at runtime;
an indexed triple store~\cite{bast2017qlever} answers a query over $10^{11}$ facts by a
join plan no engineer reconstructs. Both are trusted universally --- and the trust is manufactured not by comprehension but by
\emph{differential agreement, adversarial fuzzing, invariants, and checked projections of
the output}. We take that practice and derive it as theory, then push it to the scale where
it is not an optimization but a precondition of coherence.

\paragraph{The standard, stated once.} Let a verifier have bounded context $\CL$. A system
whose faithful description exceeds $\CL$ cannot be trusted by comprehension. We insert
between system and verifier a map whose codomain has size $\le\CL$ and whose fidelity is
guaranteed by mechanism, not by the reader. That map is the receipt; its theory is this
thesis; its slogan is \emph{beyond cognitive load, within admissible projection}.

\paragraph{Structure.} Chapter~\ref{ch:obs} axiomatizes observation and admission and
builds the admission monoid (algebra). Chapter~\ref{ch:mu} defines $\muop$, defines the
receipt as a law-bearing commitment, and proves Faithful Projection and Conservation of
Consequence \emph{in their corrected forms}. Chapter~\ref{ch:cost} separates the cost
regimes and states the Comprehension--Verification Gap. Chapter~\ref{ch:calc} develops the
attention calculus and lexicographic arbitration. Chapter~\ref{ch:geo} gives the geometry
of conformance and separability. Chapter~\ref{ch:instance} instantiates on the
$2.5$-million-token build and its trillion-agent generalization.

\chapter{Observation, Admission, and the Algebra of the Quarantine}
\label{ch:obs}

\section{First principles}

\begin{axiom}[Observation]\label{ax:obs}
There is a set $\Obs$, the \emph{observation space}, whose elements are arbitrary finite
records: logs, agent outputs, sensor frames, claims, tool responses. $\Obs$ carries no
decidable semantics: ``does this observation mean what it purports to mean'' is not assumed
computable.
\end{axiom}

\begin{theorem}[Rice, specialized]\label{thm:rice}
Let $P$ be any non-trivial semantic property of the meanings observations may encode.
Then $\{o\in\Obs : P(o)\}$ is undecidable.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Rice's theorem~\cite{rice1953}: every non-trivial property of the language recognized by a
Turing machine is undecidable. Observations are unrestricted encodings and may encode arbitrary machines;
any non-trivial semantic predicate inherits undecidability by reduction from the halting
problem. \end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:rice} is why an agent cannot admit a peer's output by deciding its
meaning. It admits by \emph{retracting} the output onto a sub-language on which the
properties of interest \emph{are} decidable.

\begin{definition}[Admitted space and admission]\label{def:adm}
Fix a decidable $\Adm\subseteq\Obs$ on which a finite battery of obligations
$g_1,\dots,g_m:\Obs\to\{0,1\}$ is computable. The \emph{admission map} is the partial
retraction
\[
\adm:\Obs\rightharpoonup\Adm\cup\{\Rfsl\},\qquad
\adm(o)=\begin{cases}\rho(o)\in\Adm & \text{if }\bigwedge_i g_i(o)=1,\\ \Rfsl & \text{else,}\end{cases}
\]
with $\rho$ a computable bounding/normalization and $\Rfsl$ the distinguished
\emph{refusal}. Admission is idempotent on its image: $\adm\circ\adm=\adm$.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
$\Rfsl$ is a first-class value. A refusal is a lawful output of a correctly functioning
admission --- an obligation failed, and the system recorded which. This is ``refusal is
data,'' and at fleet scale it is the primary signal: most inter-agent messages that matter
are refusals with reasons.
\end{remark}

\section{The admission monoid}

\begin{construction}[Denial algebra]\label{con:denial}
Let $D=(\{0,1\}^n,\lor,\bm 0)$ be the commutative idempotent monoid of \emph{denial words}:
$n$ independent lanes, componentwise OR, identity $\bm 0$. Each obligation $g_i$ contributes
a lane map $d_i:\Obs\to\{0,1\}^n$ with $d_i(o)=\bm 0\iff g_i(o)=1$. The total denial is
$d(o)=\bigvee_i d_i(o)$, and $\adm(o)\ne\Rfsl\iff d(o)=\bm 0$.
\end{construction}

\begin{proposition}[$D$ is a bounded semilattice; admission is monotone]\label{prop:semilattice}
$(\{0,1\}^n,\lor)$ is the join-semilattice of $2^{[n]}$ with bottom $\bm 0$. Adding
obligations only enlarges denials: for $G'\supseteq G$, $d_{G'}(o)\succeq d_G(o)$, so
$\{o:\adm_{G'}(o)\ne\Rfsl\}\subseteq\{o:\adm_G(o)\ne\Rfsl\}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Idempotence, commutativity, associativity of $\lor$ per coordinate; products of
semilattices are semilattices. $d_{G'}(o)=d_G(o)\lor\bigvee_{i\in G'\setminus G}d_i(o)
\succeq d_G(o)$, and $\bm 0$ is the unique minimum. \end{proof}

A refusal \emph{category} is the join-support $\supp d(o)\subseteq[n]$; the eight-bucket
taxonomy of the implementation is a fixed surjection $[n]\twoheadrightarrow\{1,\dots,8\}$.
At a trillion agents this taxonomy is a shared vocabulary: a refusal category is legible to
a recipient who never saw the sender's interior.

\chapter{Manufacture and the Law-Bearing Receipt}
\label{ch:mu}

\section{The Chatman equation}

\begin{definition}[Manufacturing morphism]\label{def:mu}
$\muop:\Adm\to\Act$ is a deterministic computable map subject to:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(M\arabic*),leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Determinism / reproducibility:} $\muop(x)$ depends only on $x$; equal admitted
inputs give bit-identical artifacts.
\item \textbf{Boundedness:} $\muop$ factors through a representation of size bounded by fixed
structural constants (arity, depth, conjuncts $\le 8$ canonically), so $\muop$ terminates
with a priori bounded cost.
\end{enumerate}
The \emph{Chatman equation} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chatman}
\Act=\muop(\Adm), \qquad\text{operationally}\qquad a=\muop(\adm(o))\ \text{ when }\adm(o)\ne\Rfsl,
\end{equation}
and $\muop(\Rfsl)=\Rfsl$: nothing is manufactured from a raw observation.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Determinism is not injectivity]
(M1) gives \emph{same input $\Rightarrow$ same output}. It does \emph{not} give
\emph{same output $\Rightarrow$ same input}: distinct admitted observations may manufacture
the identical artifact (two proposals yielding the same plan). This asymmetry is the reason
the naive uniqueness claim below must be corrected, and the reason the receipt must commit
to the observation, not merely to the artifact.
\end{remark}

\section{Receipts as law-bearing commitments}

An \emph{execution trace} $\sigma\in\Sigma$ is the full internal record of a manufacture ---
every marking, branch, token movement, sub-artifact --- of unbounded dimension. A bare hash
of $\sigma$'s bytes commits to \emph{bytes}, not to \emph{lawfulness}: it proves the trace
was not altered, not that the action was admitted, conformant, and non-refused. We therefore
define the receipt to commit to the law-bearing fields explicitly.

\begin{definition}[Law-bearing frame and receipt chain]\label{def:receipt}
Fix a collision-resistant hash $\chainH:\{0,1\}^*\to\{0,1\}^{256}$
(canonically BLAKE3~\cite{oconnor2021blake3}; the argument needs only collision resistance)
and write $\dg(\cdot)=\chainH(\cdot)$ for a digest. For a manufacture with admitted observation $x=\adm(o)$,
obligation set $G$ with denial word $d(o)$, transition identity $\tau$, artifact
$a=\muop(x)$, replay result $\Fitness$, refusal reason $r$ (empty on accept), and
implementation version $v$, the \emph{frame} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:frame}
\mathsf{fr} \;=\; \bigl\langle\, \dg(x),\; \dg(G),\; d(o),\; \tau,\; \dg(a),\;
\Fitness,\; r,\; v \,\bigr\rangle,
\end{equation}
and the \emph{chained receipt} advances by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chain}
h_{+} \;=\; \chainH\bigl(h_{-}\,\Vert\,\mathsf{fr}\bigr).
\end{equation}
The \emph{receipt projection} is
\[
\Proj:\Sigma\to\Recs,\qquad
\Proj(\sigma)=\bigl(\text{verdict},\,h_{+},\,\Fitness,\,r\bigr),
\]
a tuple of at most $\CL$ chunks: a Boolean verdict, the $256$-bit chain value, one scalar
metric, one refusal reason.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Faithful Projection]\label{thm:faithful}
Let $\sigma$ be a manufacture with law-bearing frame~\eqref{eq:frame} and chained receipt
$h_+$. The receipt is \emph{faithful to lawful consequence} exactly to the extent the frame
commits its fields: for any committed field $\phi\in\{\dg(x),\dg(G),d(o),\tau,\dg(a),\Fitness,r,v\}$,
producing a trace $\sigma'$ that differs in $\phi$ yet matches the claimed $h_+$ requires a
collision of $\chainH$. Hence under collision resistance, a verifier who recomputes
\eqref{eq:chain} rejects any such $\sigma'$ except with negligible probability. Conversely,
\emph{fields not committed by the frame are not attested}: faithfulness extends to lawfulness
only because $\dg(x),\dg(G),d(o),\Fitness,r$ are present; a frame committing bytes alone would
attest tamper-evidence but not admission.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Each field appears as an argument to $\chainH$ inside $\mathsf{fr}$, and $h_-$ commits all
predecessors recursively, so $h_+$ is a function of every committed field of the entire causal
history. If $\sigma'$ differs in a committed $\phi$ but the recomputed chain equals the claimed
$h_+$, then some application of $\chainH$ took distinct inputs to equal outputs --- a collision
--- which has negligible probability. If $\sigma'$ differs only in a field \emph{absent} from
$\mathsf{fr}$, the chain is unchanged and the difference is, correctly, not attested: the
theorem claims faithfulness precisely over committed fields, no more. The converse clause is
the contrapositive: drop $\dg(x)$ or $d(o)$ from~\eqref{eq:frame} and the same proof yields
only byte-tamper-evidence, establishing that lawfulness attestation \emph{requires} the
law-bearing commitments. \end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Comprehension is unnecessary for boundary trust]\label{cor:noncomp}
An accepting verifier's predicate factors as $\mathrm{Verify}=V\circ\Proj$ for a fixed
$V:\Recs\to\{0,1\}$ of input size $\le\CL$. Trust in the accept is a function of the
$\CL$-sized projection and the soundness of $V$, not of any agent's comprehension of $\sigma$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{theorem}[Conservation of Consequence, corrected]\label{thm:conservation}
Under Definitions~\ref{def:adm},~\ref{def:mu},~\ref{def:receipt}:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Causation.} Every actuated artifact $a$ is the image of \emph{at least one}
admitted observation: $a\in\im\muop$, and no $a\notin\im\muop$ is actuated.
\item \textbf{Position uniqueness.} Each actuation appends \emph{exactly one} receipt-chain
position; the map $\text{actuation}\mapsto h_+$ is injective up to hash collision.
\item \textbf{Observation recoverability / commitment.} These are distinct and must not be
conflated. \emph{Recoverability:} if $\muop$ is injective on $\Adm$, the artifact
$a=\muop(x)$ determines the admitted observation $x$. \emph{Commitment:} otherwise, when the
frame commits $\dg(x)$ (as in~\eqref{eq:frame}), the receipt position uniquely commits ---
under collision resistance --- to the tuple $(\dg(x),\dg(G),\tau,\dg(a))$ of
admitted-observation digest, obligation digest, transition identity, and artifact digest.
This gives \emph{commitment} uniqueness, not \emph{semantic recovery}: the receipt binds
which observation-digest was admitted, but the artifact alone need not reconstruct $x$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(i) Actuation is gated by~\eqref{eq:chatman}: $a$ actuates only if $a=\muop(x)$, $x=\adm(o)\ne
\Rfsl$; thus $\im\muop$ exhausts the actuated set and its complement never actuates. ``At least
one'' is all determinism yields, since $\muop$ need not be injective (Remark~2.2).
(ii) The chain appends one position per manufacture~\eqref{eq:chain}; distinct positions with
equal $h_+$ force a collision (Theorem~\ref{thm:faithful}).
(iii) If $\muop$ is injective on $\Adm$, then $a=\muop(x)$ determines $x$ outright ---
recoverability. Otherwise, because $\mathsf{fr}$ contains $\dg(x)$, the frame --- hence $h_+$
--- commits the observation digest; by collision resistance no second $x'$ with
$\dg(x')\ne\dg(x)$ shares the position, so the receipt uniquely \emph{commits} to the digest
tuple. This is commitment, not recovery: it binds which observation-digest was admitted
without reconstructing $x$ from $a$. \end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:conservation} is the corrected conservation law: consequence is neither
actuated without an admitted cause nor recorded without a unique receipt position, and the
\emph{observation} behind an artifact is pinned by commitment rather than by the false hope
that artifacts determine their causes.

\chapter{The Two Cost Regimes and the Comprehension--Verification Gap}
\label{ch:cost}

A recurring error is to claim that verifying an arbitrary execution is $O(1)$. It is not;
only \emph{boundary inspection} is. We separate the regimes explicitly, because at a trillion
agents the distinction decides which checks run per message and which run on audit.

\begin{definition}[Verification regimes]\label{def:regimes}
For a trace of $n$ committed frames:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Regime & Who / when & Cost\\
\midrule
Boundary projection inspection & any verifier, per message & $O(\CL)$\\
Single frame recomputation & spot check & $O(1)$ per frame\\
Checkpointed / indexed replay & sampled audit & $O(\log n)$ or bounded path\\
Full chain replay & full audit & $O(n)$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Comprehension--Verification Gap]\label{thm:gap}
Let $\mathrm{Comp}(\sigma)=\Theta(\dim\Sigma)$ be the cost of comprehending the interior and
$\mathrm{Ver}_{\partial}(\sigma)=O(\CL)$ the cost of \emph{boundary} verification of
$\Proj(\sigma)$. Then
\[
\frac{\mathrm{Comp}(\sigma)}{\mathrm{Ver}_{\partial}(\sigma)} \;=\; \Omega\!\left(\frac{\dim\Sigma}{\CL}\right)\ \xrightarrow{\ \dim\Sigma\to\infty\ }\ \infty,
\]
while boundary verification stays constant. The gap is between \emph{comprehending the
interior} and \emph{verifying the boundary projection} --- \emph{not} between trace size and
all mechanical audit: replay and full audit honestly cost $O(n)$ (Definition~\ref{def:regimes}),
reducible to $O(\log n)$ under checkpoint indexing.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
$\mathrm{Ver}_\partial$ reads $\Proj(\sigma)$ of size $\le\CL$ and applies $V$
(Corollary~\ref{cor:noncomp}): $O(\CL)$. $\mathrm{Comp}$ must ingest the interior of size
$\Theta(\dim\Sigma)$. The ratio diverges. The scope clause restates
Definition~\ref{def:regimes}: mechanical replay is $O(n)$, not $O(1)$; the constant-cost claim
is confined to boundary inspection. \end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Fleet trust budget]
Boundary verification costs $O(\CL)$ per message, independent of any agent's interior
dimension, with $O(n)$ replay reserved for sampled audit. Hence for $M$ admitted messages the
boundary trust cost is $O(M\CL)$; for a round with $O(N)$ messages (one per agent) it is
$O(N\CL)$; for all-to-all exchange among $N$ agents it is $O(N^2\CL)$. In every regime the
per-message factor is the constant $\CL$, not the interior dimension: comprehension-based trust
would replace $\CL$ by $\dim\Sigma$, giving $\Omega(M\dim\Sigma)$ and infeasibility.
Projection-based trust is what keeps an $N=10^{12}$ fleet's trust cost linear in its message
count rather than in its members' interiors.
\end{corollary}

\chapter{The Calculus of Attention and Cost}
\label{ch:calc}

\section{The attention fluent}

Scheduling admitted actions consumes a shared conserved resource, the \emph{attention fluent}
--- \emph{fluent} in Newton's original sense of a quantity that flows.

\begin{definition}[Attention balance]\label{def:attnbalance}
Let $c(t)\ge0$ be capacity and admitted action $j$ draw rate $r_j\ge0$ on $[s_j,e_j]$. Free
capacity is $f(t)=c(t)-\sum_{j:\,t\in[s_j,e_j]}r_j$. A schedule is feasible iff $f(t)\ge0$
for all $t$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Attention is conserved; makespan is the target]\label{prop:attnconservation}
Total attention $\int_0^T\!\sum_j r_j\mathbf 1_{[s_j,e_j]}(t)\,dt=\sum_j r_j(e_j-s_j)$ is
independent of ordering. Reordering redistributes \emph{when} attention is spent, not \emph{how
much}; the optimization target is the makespan functional $T[\cdot]$ under precedence and the
pointwise capacity constraint.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Linearity of the integral; each $r_j(e_j-s_j)$ depends only on $j$'s duration and rate. \end{proof}

\section{Lexicographic arbitration as an infinite-weight limit}

\begin{definition}[Cost vector]\label{def:costvector}
Assign a plan $\bm c=(c_0,c_1,\dots,c_k)$, $c_0\in\{0,1\}$ the unadmitted indicator ($0=$
admitted), $c_1,\dots,c_k$ bounded secondary costs. Order by lexicographic $\preceq_{\mathrm{lex}}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lexicographic order is the weight limit]\label{thm:lex}
With $C_\lambda(\bm c)=\sum_{i=0}^k\lambda^{k-i}c_i$, $\lambda>1$, $|c_i|\le M$: there is
$\Lambda$ with $\bm c\preceq_{\mathrm{lex}}\bm c'\iff C_\lambda(\bm c)\le C_\lambda(\bm c')$
for all $\lambda>\Lambda$. As $\lambda\to\infty$ the lawfulness coordinate's weight diverges.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If $\bm c\prec_{\mathrm{lex}}\bm c'$ they first differ at $p$ with $c'_p-c_p\ge\epsilon>0$;
then $C_\lambda(\bm c')-C_\lambda(\bm c)\ge\lambda^{k-p}\epsilon-2M\sum_{i>p}\lambda^{k-i}$,
positive once $\lambda>\Lambda=1+2Mk/\epsilon$. Ties map to equality. \end{proof}

The price of unlawfulness is not high but infinite in the limit that defines the order: no
finite secondary cost purchases an unadmitted plan.

\chapter{The Geometry of Conformance}
\label{ch:geo}

\section{The marking polytope}

Token-flow execution has a native geometry. With $p$ places, a marking is
$\bm m\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^p$; a transition $t$ is $(\bm m^-_t,\bm m^+_t)$, enabled at $\bm m$
iff $\bm m\ge\bm m^-_t$, firing to $\bm m-\bm m^-_t+\bm m^+_t$.

\begin{definition}[State equation and reachability cone]\label{def:reachcone}
Let $N\in\mathbb Z^{p\times|T|}$ have columns $\bm\delta_t=\bm m^+_t-\bm m^-_t$. A firing
sequence with Parikh vector $\bm x\ge0$ reaches $\bm m=\bm m_0+N\bm x$. The reachable set lies
in the integer points of $\bm m_0+\operatorname{cone}(N)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Conformance is membership; a fault is a separating hyperplane]\label{prop:conformembership}
A trace with Parikh vector $\bm x$ is conformant only if its marking lies in
$\mathcal P=\{\bm m_0+N\bm x:\bm x\ge\bm0\}\cap\{\bm m\ge\bm0\}$. If $\bm m^\dagger\notin\mathcal P$,
Farkas' lemma~\cite{schrijver1986} yields $\bm y$ with $\bm y^\top N\le\bm0$ and $\bm y^\top(\bm m^\dagger-\bm m_0)>0$:
a certificate of nonconformance.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Membership is the state equation with $\bm x\ge0$; Farkas applied to $N\bm x=\bm m^\dagger-
\bm m_0,\ \bm x\ge0$ gives either a nonnegative solution or the separating $\bm y$. \end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Fitness as normalized distance]\label{def:fitnessdistance}
$\Fitness(\sigma)=1-\dfrac{\text{tokens consumed on unenabled transitions}}{\text{tokens the
replay attempted to consume}}\in[0,1]$; $\Fitness=1$ iff the replay never forced a disabled
firing, and $1-\Fitness$ is a normalized $\ell^1$ excursion outside the enabled set.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Unit fitness characterizes lawful traces; the fault localizes]\label{thm:unitfitness}
A trace is a firing sequence iff $\Fitness=1$. If $\Fitness<1$, the first frame forcing an
unenabled consumption is a coordinate-localized witness $t^\star$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Enabled firings never force consumption, giving numerator $0$ and, inductively, a genuine
firing sequence within $\mathcal P$. A disabled event records a forced consumption at the
earliest index $t^\star$, making the numerator positive. \end{proof}

\section{Separability is a Rice quarantine for processes}

\begin{theorem}[Separable decomposition, after Kourani--van der Aalst~\cite{kourani2026,vanderaalst2016}]\label{thm:sep}
A sound, separable workflow net admits a recursive decomposition into a POWL~2.0 model whose
every node is a choice graph (all exclusive/cyclic logic) or a partial order (all concurrent
logic); each node's local marking geometry has dimension bounded by its arity, independent of
global net size.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
Theorem~\ref{thm:sep} is admission for processes: separable nets decompose into bounded lanes
(the algorithm certifies membership); non-separable nets are \emph{refused with reason}.
Separability is the decidable sub-language onto which arbitrary workflow logic is retracted,
exactly as $\Adm$ retracts $\Obs$. The interior is high-dimensional; each lane is bounded; the
boundary certificate is small --- the projection principle, in geometry.
\end{remark}

\chapter{The Instance: From One Build to a Trillion Agents}
\label{ch:instance}

\section{The build no agent read}

Over a bounded interval an autonomous fleet performed exploration, planning, and
implementation across a constellation of repositories. Its interior consumed
$\Sigma_{\text{tokens}}\approx 2.5\times 10^{6}$ machine-generated tokens that no agent read
in full. Its trustworthiness rests entirely on boundary receipts: the whole interior projects
to coordinates of the form
\[
\bigl(\text{verdict}=\textsf{pass},\ h_+=\texttt{<256-bit hex>},\ \Fitness=1.0,\ \text{refusals}=k\ \text{(each receipted)}\bigr),
\]
plus a coverage figure $\text{pass\_rate}\in[0,1]$ over an enumerated frontier and a
differential-oracle agreement flag. By Theorem~\ref{thm:faithful} each is faithful over its
committed fields; by Theorem~\ref{thm:gap} each is verifiable at $O(\CL)$; the refusals are not
omissions but materialized join-supports of the denial monoid (Proposition~\ref{prop:semilattice}).

\section{The trillion-agent projection}

The headline construction. Here the theory stops being an audit method for one build and
becomes a control plane for a civilization of bounded agents. We are scrupulous about which
statements are theorems, which are arithmetic, and which are engineering estimates or
consequences --- the distinction is the antibody, and at this scale it is easy to lose.

\begin{construction}[Agent as eight bits]\label{con:agent8}
\emph{(Construction --- definitional.)} Project each agent to a status byte $b\in\{0,1\}^8$
whose lanes name \textsf{admitted}, \textsf{evidence-ok}, \textsf{within-budget},
\textsf{authority-bound}, \textsf{healthy}, \textsf{conformant}, \textsf{receipted},
\textsf{replayable}. A fleet of $N$ agents is a bit-packed vector; a $64$-bit word holds $8$
agents; a fleet admission sweep is the branchless mask reduction of
Construction~\ref{con:denial} applied word-parallel. An agent does not ask to be understood.
It presents a lawful projection.
\end{construction}

\subsection*{What the status byte is not}

The byte is \emph{not} an agent self-report. It is a mechanism-computed projection
(Definition~\ref{def:receipt}) of the agent's receipted lifecycle: each lane is \emph{set only
by} the corresponding mechanism, never by the agent's assertion about itself.
\textsf{Admitted} is set by the admission retraction $\adm$ (Definition~\ref{def:adm}), not by
a claim of admissibility; \textsf{evidence-ok} by satisfied obligations $g_i$;
\textsf{conformant} by a replay with $\Fitness=1$ (Chapter~\ref{ch:geo}); \textsf{receipted}
and \textsf{replayable} only by chained receipts whose frames commit the law-bearing
fields~\eqref{eq:frame}; the denial word $d(o)$ (Construction~\ref{con:denial}) forces the
complementary lanes. Thus the byte is faithful by Theorem~\ref{thm:faithful}, and small enough
that a peer with context $\CL$ reads a counterpart's entire lawful standing in one glance ---
\emph{a glance at a computed coordinate, not at a claim}. Each lane is fully mechanical:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Lane & Set by (mechanism) & Refused by\\
\midrule
\textsf{admitted} & $\adm(o)\ne\Rfsl$ & denial word $d(o)\ne\bm 0$\\
\textsf{evidence-ok} & obligation battery $\bigwedge_i g_i$ passes & missing/failed evidence\\
\textsf{within-budget} & cost fluent within bound & budget overflow\\
\textsf{authority-bound} & authority/policy digest satisfied & authority mismatch\\
\textsf{healthy} & lifecycle / heartbeat receipt & failed health check\\
\textsf{conformant} & replay with $\Fitness=1$ & disabled transition ($\Fitness<1$)\\
\textsf{receipted} & valid chain position~\eqref{eq:chain} & missing / broken receipt\\
\textsf{replayable} & replay index / checkpoint exists & unreplayable artifact\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The byte is not an ontology of the agent. It is the \emph{local} lawful status projection
required for a given admission surface: a different surface (a different obligation battery,
a different policy) computes a different byte from the same agent. Locality is a feature ---
the projection is exactly as wide as the interaction demands, and no wider.

\subsection*{What this theory refuses to trust}

Symmetrically, the following carry \emph{no} standing under this theory, because none is a
committed field of a faithful receipt frame~\eqref{eq:frame}. Each is admissible only as raw
observation $o\in\Obs$ (Axiom~\ref{ax:obs}), to be quarantined and admitted or refused ---
never taken as attested:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
\item an agent's \textbf{self-report} of its own status or success;
\item an \textbf{LLM explanation} or rationale offered in place of a receipt;
\item a \textbf{natural-language trace} of what was ``done'';
\item \textbf{uncommitted metadata} --- any field absent from $\mathsf{fr}$ (by the converse
      clause of Theorem~\ref{thm:faithful}, it is not attested);
\item an \textbf{unchecked policy/implementation version} not committed as $v$;
\item an \textbf{unreplayed artifact} whose $\Fitness$ was never computed;
\item any \textbf{unreceipted claim} (by the antibody proposition below, it is in
      $\mathrm{Over}$ and rejected).
\end{itemize}
Trust attaches to committed fields and computed lanes; everything else is observation awaiting
admission.

\begin{proposition}[Fleet arithmetic and its estimates]\label{prop:planetary}
For a fleet of $N$ agents under Construction~\ref{con:agent8}:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{(Arithmetic --- exact.)} The status vector occupies exactly $N$ bytes; at
      $N=10^{12}$ that is $1\,\mathrm{TB}$, packed to $N/8$ $64$-bit words. A full admission
      sweep is $N/8$ masked-OR word operations.
\item \textbf{(Engineering estimate.)} Compressed to per-byte lane entropy under typical
      sparsity, status is $\sim\!10\,\mathrm{GB}$; at streaming bandwidth $B$ a sweep takes
      $\Theta(N/(8B))$ wall-clock.
\item \textbf{(Order-of-magnitude estimate.)} The affordability ratio between one cache-line
      mask op ($\sim$ns) and one LLM admission decision ($\sim\!10^{-2}\,$s) is $\sim\!10^{7}$.
\item \textbf{(Consequence, not theorem.)} Therefore a comprehension-based or per-agent-LLM
      planetary control plane is infeasible by $\sim\!N\cdot 10^{7}$, while a bit-parallel
      admission sweep is feasible; planetary coherence of a bounded-agent fleet is available
      only through projection-based admission.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[On the labels]
(a) is counting. (b),(c) are estimates and are marked as such; they depend on hardware and
workload and are not claimed as theorems. (d) is the engineering consequence of composing (a)
with the Fleet Trust Budget corollary of Chapter~\ref{ch:cost}: per-message cost $O(\CL)$
versus $\Omega(\dim\Sigma)$ for comprehension, scaled by $N$. No part of this proposition
asserts a mathematical impossibility; it asserts an economic separation under stated
estimates. \end{proof}

\section{A worked status byte}

To make the projection concrete, we compute one agent's status byte from its receipts.
Order the lanes as in Construction~\ref{con:agent8}, most significant first:
$b=(\textsf{adm},\textsf{ev},\textsf{bud},\textsf{auth},\textsf{hlth},\textsf{conf},\textsf{rcpt},\textsf{repl})$.
An agent has just completed a contract-claim manufacture. Its receipt chain contains a frame
$\mathsf{fr}=\langle\dg(x),\dg(G),d(o),\tau,\dg(a),\Fitness,r,v\rangle$ with the following
mechanism outcomes:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
\item $\adm(o)\ne\Rfsl$ and $d(o)=\bm 0$: admission passed $\Rightarrow \textsf{adm}=1$.
\item obligation battery $\bigwedge_i g_i=1$ (signature and ledger evidence present)
      $\Rightarrow \textsf{ev}=1$.
\item cost fluent within its bound $\Rightarrow \textsf{bud}=1$.
\item the authority digest committed as part of policy $v$ is satisfied
      $\Rightarrow \textsf{auth}=1$.
\item lifecycle heartbeat receipt current $\Rightarrow \textsf{hlth}=1$.
\item token replay of the lifecycle trace returns $\Fitness=1$
      $\Rightarrow \textsf{conf}=1$.
\item a valid chain position $h_+$ exists~\eqref{eq:chain} $\Rightarrow \textsf{rcpt}=1$.
\item a replay checkpoint index exists $\Rightarrow \textsf{repl}=1$.
\end{itemize}
Hence $b = \mathtt{1111\,1111} = \mathtt{0xFF}$: the agent presents as fully lawful on this
surface. A peer reads one byte and, by the derivation table, knows precisely which mechanism
set each bit --- no interior, no self-report. Now suppose the same agent's ledger evidence is
absent: then $g_{\text{ledger}}=0$ forces a denial lane, $\textsf{ev}=0$, and (because
admission requires all obligations) $\textsf{adm}=0$, giving $b=\mathtt{0011\,1111}=\mathtt{0x3F}$
with the refusal category $\supp d(o)=\{\textsf{Prerequisites}\}$ attached. The peer reads
\texttt{0x3F} and the category, and refuses to depend on the agent for this surface --- again
from a computed coordinate, not a claim.

\section{What this refuses to trust: five adversaries}

Each adversary attempts to obtain standing without a faithful receipt; each is defeated by a
named mechanism, not by inspection of the interior.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Forged self-report.} An agent emits ``\textsf{healthy}=1, \textsf{conformant}=1''
      as a message. \emph{Defeated:} the byte is not read from the message; it is recomputed
      from the agent's receipt chain (the derivation table). An assertion is raw observation
      $o\in\Obs$ (Axiom~\ref{ax:obs}), admitted or refused, never attested.
\item \textbf{Broken replay.} An artifact is presented with $\textsf{conf}=1$ but its trace
      contains a disabled firing. \emph{Defeated:} token replay yields $\Fitness<1$ and
      localizes the first offending transition $t^\star$ (Chapter~\ref{ch:geo}); the
      \textsf{conf} lane is forced to $0$.
\item \textbf{Missing policy digest.} An action claims authority under a policy version $v$ the
      frame never committed. \emph{Defeated:} by the converse clause of
      Theorem~\ref{thm:faithful}, an uncommitted field is not attested; $\textsf{auth}$ cannot
      be set, and the claim is in $\mathrm{Over}$.
\item \textbf{Non-separable workflow.} A process is submitted whose net does not decompose.
      \emph{Defeated:} the separability admission (Theorem~\ref{thm:sep}, remark) refuses it
      with a stated reason rather than admitting an unbounded lane.
\item \textbf{Unreceipted claim.} A result is asserted with no chain position.
      \emph{Defeated:} $\textsf{rcpt}=0$; by the antibody proposition below the claim is in
      $\mathrm{Over}$ and rejected under the \textsf{docs-exceed-mechanism} invariant.
\end{enumerate}
In each case the defense is a mechanism that sets or refuses a committed field --- the theory's
consistent answer to ``why should I believe you'' is ``you have a faithful receipt, or you do
not.''

\section{The antibody clause, formalized}

\begin{proposition}[Self-audited scale]\label{prop:selfauditedscale}
Let a system emit claims $c$ with receipts $r(c)$, and define the overclaim set
$\mathrm{Over}=\{c:\nexists\,r(c)\text{ with }V(\Proj)=1\}$. If the system enforces
$\mathrm{Over}=\varnothing$ (the \textsf{docs-exceed-mechanism} defect class), the admissible
magnitude of a claim is bounded by the mechanism that receipts its limits, not by rhetoric.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Every admissible claim has a faithful receipt, verifiable at $O(\CL)$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:gap});
a claim lacking one is in $\mathrm{Over}$ and rejected by the invariant. Admissible claims are
exactly receipted claims. \end{proof}

This is the doctrine's guardrail: \emph{a grand system without receipts is grandiosity; a grand
system that receipts its own overclaims is machinery.} It is what licenses a thesis addressed to
a trillion agents to make large claims --- each is receipted, or it is refused.

\chapter{Conclusion}

We justified, rather than assumed, the trust that bounded agents must place in systems and
peers they cannot comprehend. Admission retracts undecidable observation onto a decidable
sub-language (Chapter~\ref{ch:obs}); a deterministic bounded morphism manufactures artifacts
from admitted observations (Chapter~\ref{ch:mu}); a chained receipt \emph{committing law-bearing
fields} projects the unbounded trace onto a $\le\CL$ coordinate faithful over exactly what it
commits (Faithful Projection, corrected). We separated the cost regimes so the doctrine claims
only what is true: boundary verification is $O(\CL)$, replay is honestly $O(n)$
(Chapter~\ref{ch:cost}). We corrected Conservation of Consequence to \emph{at-least-one} cause
and \emph{unique receipt position}, distinguishing observation \emph{recoverability} (under
injective $\muop$) from observation \emph{commitment} (under digest-committing frames) ---
the receipt binds which observation-digest was admitted, which is not the same as
reconstructing the observation (Chapter~\ref{ch:mu}). The attention calculus prices unlawfulness at infinity
(Chapter~\ref{ch:calc}); the marking geometry makes conformance membership with Farkas
certificates and localizes faults (Chapter~\ref{ch:geo}); separability is the process-layer
quarantine. The instance scales from one $2.5$-million-token build to a $10^{12}$-agent fleet
each projected to a faithful byte (Chapter~\ref{ch:instance}).

The standard was never comprehension. Among a trillion agents it could not be. The standard is a
faithful projection, small enough to hold and guaranteed by mechanism. An agent does not ask to
be understood; it presents a lawful projection, and its peers verify the projection, not the
agent.

\begin{center}\itshape
Beyond cognitive load; within admissible projection.\\[0.3em]
At trillion-agent scale, trust is not understanding. Trust is receipted projection.
\end{center}
