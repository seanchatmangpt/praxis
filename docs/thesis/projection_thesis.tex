\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
We develop, from first principles, a theory for trusting computational systems whose
internal state and control flow exceed any human observer's working-memory capacity.
The central object is the \emph{Chatman equation} $\Act = \muop(\Adm)$, read as:
an artifact is the lawful image, under a deterministic manufacturing morphism $\muop$,
of an \emph{admitted} observation. We formalize admission as a retraction onto a
decidable sub-language (the \emph{Rice quarantine}), define a \emph{receipt} as a
projection $\Proj$ from an arbitrarily high-dimensional execution trace onto a
bounded coordinate, and prove three results: (i) the \emph{Faithful Projection
Theorem}, that a collision-resistant chained receipt lets a bounded verifier reject
any perturbed execution with overwhelming probability while reading only
$O(1)$ symbols; (ii) the \emph{Comprehension--Verification Gap}, a quantitative
statement that verification cost is bounded by the projection dimension and is
independent of the interior dimension; and (iii) a \emph{Conservation Law of
Consequence}, that under the admission discipline every actuated effect is the image
of exactly one admitted observation and carries a unique receipt. We give the algebra
of the admission monoid, the calculus of the cost/attention fluent that governs
scheduling, and the geometry of the token-flow state space in which conformance is a
distance-to-a-polytope. The thesis closes by exhibiting a concrete instance --- a
seven-day autonomous build that spent on the order of $2.5\times 10^{6}$ machine-generated
tokens no human read --- and showing that its trustworthiness rests entirely on
boundary receipts sized for a human, not on comprehension of the interior.

\chapter{Introduction: The Standard Was Never Comprehension}

A persistent assumption in engineering practice holds that a trustworthy system must
be one a competent human can hold in mind. This assumption is false for the systems
that matter most, and the falsity is not incidental but structural. A branchless
SIMD document parser classifies sixty-four input bytes per vector instruction through
carry-less arithmetic that no engineer traces mentally at runtime; a triple store
answers a query over $10^{11}$ facts by walking permutation indexes whose join plan
no engineer reconstructs by hand. Both are trusted universally. The trust does not
arise from comprehension. It arises from \emph{differential agreement, adversarial
fuzzing, invariants, and checked projections of the output}.

This thesis takes that observed practice and derives it as a theorem. We fix, once,
the quantity that makes the problem well posed: human working memory admits a small
constant number of simultaneously manipulable chunks, which we write $\CL$ and take,
following the cognitive-load literature, to be a fixed small integer (canonically
$\CL \approx 4$). A system whose faithful description requires more than $\CL$ chunks
cannot be verified by direct comprehension. We do not respond by shrinking the system.
We respond by inserting, between the system and the human, a map whose codomain has
dimension at most $\CL$ and whose fidelity is guaranteed by the mechanism rather than
by the reader. That map is the receipt, and this thesis is its theory.

\paragraph{Contributions and structure.}
Chapter~\ref{ch:obs} axiomatizes observation, admission, and the Rice quarantine, and
constructs the admission monoid (algebra). Chapter~\ref{ch:mu} defines the
manufacturing morphism $\muop$ and the receipt projection $\Proj$, and proves the
Faithful Projection Theorem and the Conservation Law. Chapter~\ref{ch:calc} develops
the calculus of the attention fluent and derives lexicographic cost arbitration as a
limit of a weighted objective. Chapter~\ref{ch:geo} gives the geometry: the marking
polytope, conformance as distance, and separability as a decomposition of that
geometry. Chapter~\ref{ch:instance} instantiates the theory on the $2.5$-million-token
build and states the Comprehension--Verification Gap as a corollary with measured
constants.

\chapter{Observation, Admission, and the Algebra of the Quarantine}
\label{ch:obs}

\section{First principles}

We begin with the least structure that makes ``lawful action'' expressible.

\begin{axiom}[Observation]\label{ax:obs}
There is a set $\Obs$, the \emph{observation space}, whose elements are arbitrary
finite records: logs, model outputs, sensor frames, human claims, tool responses.
$\Obs$ carries no decidable semantics: the map ``does this observation mean what it
purports to mean'' is not assumed computable.
\end{axiom}

Axiom~\ref{ax:obs} is a modeling commitment, not a limitation, and it is forced by a
classical result.

\begin{theorem}[Rice, specialized]\label{thm:rice}
Let $P$ be any non-trivial semantic property of the programs (equivalently, of the
meanings) that observations in $\Obs$ may encode. Then $\{o\in\Obs : P(o)\}$ is
undecidable.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Rice's theorem states that every non-trivial property of the language recognized by a
Turing machine is undecidable. Observations are unrestricted finite encodings and may
encode arbitrary machines; any non-trivial semantic predicate on them therefore inherits
undecidability by the standard reduction from the halting problem. \end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:rice} is the reason the whole apparatus exists: one cannot admit an
observation by deciding its semantics. One admits it by \emph{retracting} it onto a
sub-language on which the properties of interest \emph{are} decidable.

\begin{definition}[Admitted space and admission]\label{def:adm}
Fix a decidable sub-collection $\Adm \subseteq \Obs$ --- the \emph{admitted space} ---
on which a finite battery of obligations $g_1,\dots,g_m : \Obs \to \{0,1\}$ is
computable. The \emph{admission map} is the partial retraction
\[
\adm : \Obs \rightharpoonup \Adm \cup \{\Rfsl\},\qquad
\adm(o) =
\begin{cases}
\rho(o) \in \Adm & \text{if } \textstyle\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m} g_i(o) = 1,\\[2pt]
\Rfsl & \text{otherwise,}
\end{cases}
\]
where $\rho:\Obs\rightharpoonup\Adm$ is a computable bounding/normalization and $\Rfsl$
is the distinguished \emph{refusal}. Admission is idempotent on its image:
$\adm\circ\adm = \adm$ (a retraction).
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
$\Rfsl$ is a first-class value, not an exception. A refusal is a lawful output of a
correctly functioning admission: the system computed that an obligation failed and
recorded which one. This is the formal content of ``refusal is data.''
\end{remark}

\section{The admission monoid}

Obligation checking composes, and the composition has algebraic structure we will use
in the geometry chapter.

\begin{construction}[Denial algebra]\label{con:denial}
Let $D = (\{0,1\}^{n}, \lor, \bm{0})$ be the commutative idempotent monoid of
\emph{denial words}: $n$ independent lanes, componentwise OR, identity $\bm{0}$
(``admitted, all lanes clear''). Each obligation $g_i$ contributes a lane map
$d_i:\Obs\to\{0,1\}^n$ with $d_i(o) = \bm 0 \iff g_i(o)=1$. The total denial of an
observation is
\[
d(o) \;=\; \bigvee_{i=1}^{m} d_i(o) \;\in\; \{0,1\}^n,
\qquad \adm(o)\neq\Rfsl \iff d(o)=\bm 0 .
\]
\end{construction}

\begin{proposition}[$D$ is a bounded semilattice]\label{prop:semilattice}
$(\{0,1\}^n,\lor)$ is a commutative idempotent monoid; equivalently the join-semilattice
of the Boolean lattice $2^{[n]}$ with bottom $\bm 0$. Consequently admission is monotone:
adding obligations can only enlarge the denial (turn admits into refusals), never the
reverse.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Idempotence $x\lor x = x$, commutativity, and associativity are the defining laws of
$\lor$ on each coordinate; the product of semilattices is a semilattice. Monotonicity:
if $G'\supseteq G$ are obligation sets then $d_{G'}(o) = d_G(o)\lor\bigvee_{i\in
G'\setminus G} d_i(o) \succeq d_G(o)$ in the coordinatewise order, and $d(o)=\bm0$ is
the unique minimum, so $\{o: d_{G'}(o)=\bm0\}\subseteq\{o: d_G(o)=\bm0\}$. \end{proof}

Proposition~\ref{prop:semilattice} is the algebraic form of ``the quarantine only ever
tightens.'' It also gives the refusal taxonomy its type: a refusal \emph{category} is
the join-irreducible support $\supp d(o)\subseteq[n]$, and the eight-bucket taxonomy of
the implementation is a fixed surjection $[n]\twoheadrightarrow \{1,\dots,8\}$ onto
named categories.

\chapter{Manufacture and the Receipt Projection}
\label{ch:mu}

\section{The Chatman equation}

\begin{definition}[Manufacturing morphism]\label{def:mu}
A \emph{manufacturing morphism} is a deterministic computable map
$\muop : \Adm \to \Act$ from admitted observations to artifacts, subject to two laws:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(M\arabic*),leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Determinism / reproducibility:} $\muop(x)$ depends only on $x$; equal
        admitted inputs yield bit-identical artifacts.
  \item \textbf{Boundedness:} $\muop$ factors through a representation of size bounded
        by fixed structural constants (arity, depth, conjunct count $\le 8$ in the
        canonical profile), so that $\muop$ terminates and its cost is a priori bounded.
\end{enumerate}
The governing identity, the \emph{Chatman equation}, is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chatman}
\Act \;=\; \muop(\Adm), \qquad\text{operationally}\qquad
a \;=\; \muop(\adm(o)) \ \text{ whenever } \adm(o)\neq\Rfsl .
\end{equation}
Nothing is manufactured from a raw observation; only from an admitted one. Equivalently,
$\muop$ is undefined on $\Obs\setminus\Adm$ by construction, and $\Rfsl$ propagates:
$\muop(\Rfsl)=\Rfsl$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Functoriality of manufacture]
If admitted observations form a category $\mathbf{Adm}$ whose morphisms are the
lawful edits $\delta:\; x\to x\sqcup\delta$ (join with an admitted increment), and
artifacts form $\mathbf{Art}$ under composition of edits, then a deterministic $\muop$
with $\muop(x\sqcup\delta)=\muop(x)\ast\muop_\delta$ for a partial action $\ast$ is a
functor $\mathbf{Adm}\to\mathbf{Art}$. Composition of manufactures is manufacture of
composition: $\muop_2\circ\muop_1$ manufactures the composite artifact.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Identity edit maps to identity artifact by (M1); associativity of $\ast$ follows from
determinism applied to the associative $\sqcup$ on admitted increments. Hence the
assignment on objects and edits preserves identities and composition, i.e.\ is a
functor. \end{proof}

\section{Receipts as projections}

Let an \emph{execution trace} be the full internal record of a manufacture: every
intermediate marking, branch, token movement, and sub-artifact. Traces live in a space
$\Sigma$ of astronomically high --- indeed unbounded --- dimension. A human verifier has
capacity $\CL$. The receipt is the map that reconciles the two.

\begin{definition}[Receipt projection]\label{def:receipt}
Fix a collision-resistant hash $\chainH:\{0,1\}^*\to\{0,1\}^{256}$. For a trace
$\sigma\in\Sigma$ with payload bytes $b(\sigma)$, previous chain value $h_{-}$, and
metadata $\theta$ (instruction index, activity, timestamp, denial word), the
\emph{frame} is a fixed-width encoding $\mathsf{fr}(\sigma) =
\langle \theta, \chainH(b(\sigma))\rangle$ and the \emph{chained receipt} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chain}
h_{+} \;=\; \chainH\bigl(h_{-} \,\Vert\, \mathsf{fr}(\sigma)\bigr).
\end{equation}
The \emph{receipt projection} is
\[
\Proj : \Sigma \longrightarrow \Rec, \qquad
\Proj(\sigma) = \bigl(\,\text{verdict},\; h_{+},\; \Fitness(\sigma),\; \text{reason}\,\bigr),
\]
a tuple of at most $\CL$ chunks: a Boolean verdict, the $256$-bit chain value, one
scalar metric $\Fitness$, and one refusal reason (empty on accept).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Faithful Projection]\label{thm:faithful}
Let $\sigma$ be an admitted execution and $h_+$ its chained receipt~\eqref{eq:chain}.
Suppose an adversary presents a perturbed trace $\sigma'\neq\sigma$ agreeing with
$\sigma$ on the claimed receipt $h_+$. Then producing such a $\sigma'$ requires
exhibiting a collision of $\chainH$; hence under the collision resistance of $\chainH$,
a verifier who recomputes~\eqref{eq:chain} from $\sigma'$ and compares $256$ bits
rejects $\sigma'$ except with probability negligible in the security parameter.
Moreover the verifier reads $O(1)$ symbols --- exactly the $\CL$-chunk projection ---
independent of $\dim\Sigma$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The chain~\eqref{eq:chain} commits to $b(\sigma)$ through $\chainH(b(\sigma))$ inside
$\mathsf{fr}$, and to all predecessors through $h_-$, which itself commits recursively.
Thus $h_+$ is a function of the entire causal history. If $\sigma'\neq\sigma$ but the
recomputed chain equals the claimed $h_+$, then either some $\chainH(b(\sigma'))
=\chainH(b(\sigma))$ with $b(\sigma')\neq b(\sigma)$ (a collision of $\chainH$), or the
recomputation differs and the $256$-bit comparison fails. By collision resistance the
first event has negligible probability. The verifier's work is one hash recomputation
per frame it chooses to check and one $256$-bit equality; to \emph{reject} it suffices
to find the first frame whose recomputation disagrees, and to \emph{accept} the
projection it reads only $(\text{verdict},h_+,\Fitness,\text{reason})$, a quantity of
size independent of $\dim\Sigma$. \end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Comprehension is unnecessary]\label{cor:noncomp}
Trust in an accepted artifact is a function of $\Proj(\sigma)$ and the soundness of the
verifier, not of any human's comprehension of $\sigma$. Formally, the verifier's accept
predicate factors as $\mathrm{Verify} = V\circ\Proj$ for a fixed $V:\Rec\to\{0,1\}$ of
input size $\le\CL$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{theorem}[Conservation of Consequence]\label{thm:conservation}
Under Definitions~\ref{def:adm}, \ref{def:mu}, \ref{def:receipt}, every actuated
artifact $a\in\im\muop$ is the image of exactly one admitted observation and is
committed by exactly one receipt chain position; and no $a\notin\im\muop$ is actuated.
Consequently the map $a\mapsto h_+(a)$ from actuated artifacts to receipt positions is
injective up to hash collision.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Actuation is gated: by~\eqref{eq:chatman}, $a$ is actuated only if $a=\muop(x)$ for some
$x=\adm(o)\neq\Rfsl$; hence $\im\muop$ exhausts the actuated set and its complement is
never actuated. Determinism (M1) makes $x\mapsto\muop(x)$ a function, and the chain
appends exactly one position per manufacture, so each actuation occupies a unique
chain index. Injectivity up to collision is Theorem~\ref{thm:faithful} applied to the
frames committing $b(\muop(x))$. \end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:conservation} is the ``conservation law'': consequence is neither
created without an admitted cause nor destroyed without a receipt, and the chain
preserves order. It is the discrete analogue of a continuity equation, made precise in
the next chapter.

\chapter{The Calculus of Attention and Cost}
\label{ch:calc}

\section{The attention fluent}

Scheduling admitted actions consumes a shared, conserved resource we call the
\emph{attention fluent} $\Fitness_a(t)$ --- the operator/agent capacity available at
time $t$. Borrowing the term \emph{fluent} in its original Newtonian sense (a quantity
that flows), we model it as a continuous state variable obeying a balance law.

\begin{definition}[Attention balance]
Let $c(t)\ge 0$ be attention capacity and let admitted action $j$ draw rate $r_j\ge0$
while active on an interval $[s_j,e_j]$. The instantaneous free capacity is
\[
f(t) \;=\; c(t) \;-\; \sum_{j:\, t\in[s_j,e_j]} r_j .
\]
A schedule is \emph{feasible} iff $f(t)\ge 0$ for all $t$, i.e.\ the conservation
constraint $\sum_j r_j \mathbf{1}_{[s_j,e_j]}(t)\le c(t)$ holds pointwise.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Makespan as a functional]
For a fixed feasible schedule the makespan $T=\max_j e_j$ is the terminal time of the
capacity balance, and the total attention consumed is the integral
\[
\int_0^T \Bigl(\,\sum_j r_j\mathbf 1_{[s_j,e_j]}(t)\Bigr)\,dt
= \sum_j r_j\,(e_j-s_j),
\]
a conserved quantity independent of the schedule's ordering. Thus reordering
redistributes \emph{when} attention is spent but not \emph{how much}; the optimization
target is the makespan functional $T[\,\cdot\,]$, minimized subject to precedence and
the pointwise capacity constraint.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Linearity of the integral gives the second identity; the first is the definition of
makespan. Independence of ordering follows because each term $r_j(e_j-s_j)$ depends
only on action $j$'s duration and rate, not on its placement. \end{proof}

\section{Lexicographic arbitration as a limit}

When several admitted plans compete, the implementation orders them by a
\emph{cost vector} with lawfulness dominating. We derive this lexicographic order as a
limit of ordinary weighted optimization, which explains why ``admitted'' is not merely
a large weight but an infinite one.

\begin{definition}[Cost vector]
To a candidate plan assign $\bm{c} = (c_0,c_1,\dots,c_k)$ with $c_0\in\{0,1\}$ the
\emph{unadmitted} indicator ($0$ = admitted) and $c_1,\dots,c_k$ the ordered secondary
costs (mutation risk, attention-seconds, tokens, latency, switches). Order plans by the
lexicographic order $\preceq_{\mathrm{lex}}$ on $\bm c$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lexicographic order is the weight limit]\label{thm:lex}
Let $C_\lambda(\bm c) = \sum_{i=0}^{k}\lambda^{\,k-i} c_i$ for $\lambda>1$, and suppose
all secondary costs are bounded, $|c_i|\le M$. Then there exists $\Lambda$ such that for
all $\lambda>\Lambda$,
\[
\bm c \preceq_{\mathrm{lex}} \bm c' \iff C_\lambda(\bm c)\le C_\lambda(\bm c') .
\]
In particular as $\lambda\to\infty$ the weighted objective $C_\lambda$ induces exactly
the lexicographic order, and the weight on the lawfulness coordinate $c_0$ diverges.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If $\bm c\prec_{\mathrm{lex}}\bm c'$ they first differ at some index $p$ with
$c_p<c'_p$, so $c'_p-c_p\ge \epsilon$ for the (discrete or bounded) resolution
$\epsilon>0$. Then
\[
C_\lambda(\bm c')-C_\lambda(\bm c)
= \lambda^{k-p}(c'_p-c_p) + \sum_{i>p}\lambda^{k-i}(c'_i-c_i)
\ge \lambda^{k-p}\epsilon - 2M\sum_{i>p}\lambda^{k-i}.
\]
The positive term is order $\lambda^{k-p}$ and the tail is $O(\lambda^{k-p-1})$, so the
difference is positive once $\lambda>\Lambda:=1+2M(k)/\epsilon$. Ties ($\bm c=\bm c'$)
map to equality. Hence the induced orders coincide for $\lambda>\Lambda$; letting
$\lambda\to\infty$ the lawfulness coordinate's weight $\lambda^{k}$ dominates all
others, which is the statement that no finite secondary cost can purchase an unadmitted
plan. \end{proof}

Theorem~\ref{thm:lex} is the precise sense of ``you cannot buy your way past the first
letter'': the price of unlawfulness is not high but infinite in the limit that defines
the order.

\chapter{The Geometry of Conformance}
\label{ch:geo}

\section{The marking polytope}

Token-flow execution --- Petri nets, POWL, the lifecycle typestate --- has a native
geometry. Let there be $p$ places; a \emph{marking} is $\bm m\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge0}^{p}$,
and a transition $t$ is a pair $(\bm m^-_t,\bm m^+_t)$ of pre- and post-conditions.
Firing $t$ at $\bm m$ is defined iff $\bm m\ge\bm m^-_t$ (coordinatewise) and yields
$\bm m' = \bm m - \bm m^-_t + \bm m^+_t$.

\begin{definition}[Reachability cone and state equation]
Stack the transition displacements $\bm\delta_t=\bm m^+_t-\bm m^-_t$ as columns of the
\emph{incidence matrix} $N\in\mathbb Z^{p\times|T|}$. If a firing sequence fires each
transition $t$ a total of $x_t\ge0$ times (the \emph{Parikh vector} $\bm x$), then any
reachable marking satisfies the \emph{state equation}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:stateeq}
\bm m \;=\; \bm m_0 + N\bm x, \qquad \bm x\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^{|T|} .
\end{equation}
The set of $\bm m$ satisfying~\eqref{eq:stateeq} for some $\bm x\ge0$ is the integer
points of the translated cone $\bm m_0 + \operatorname{cone}(N)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Conformance is membership; nonconformance is a separating hyperplane]
A trace with Parikh vector $\bm x$ is language-conformant only if its induced marking
lies in the polytope $\mathcal P=\{\bm m_0+N\bm x : \bm x\ge \bm 0\}\cap\{\bm m\ge\bm0\}$.
If an observed marking $\bm m^\dagger\notin\mathcal P$, then by the Farkas lemma there
exists $\bm y$ with $\bm y^\top N\le \bm 0$ and $\bm y^\top(\bm m^\dagger-\bm m_0)>0$:
a certificate (separating hyperplane) of nonconformance.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Membership in the finitely generated cone is the state equation with $\bm x\ge0$.
Farkas' lemma applied to the system $N\bm x = \bm m^\dagger-\bm m_0,\ \bm x\ge0$ yields
either a nonnegative solution (conformance) or a vector $\bm y$ with $\bm y^\top N\le0$
and $\bm y^\top(\bm m^\dagger-\bm m_0)>0$ (the certificate). The two alternatives are
mutually exclusive, which is exactly a decision with a proof. \end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Conformance fitness as normalized distance]
Let $\Fitness(\sigma) = 1 - \dfrac{\text{tokens consumed on unenabled transitions}}
{\text{tokens the replay attempted to consume}}\in[0,1]$. Then $\Fitness=1$ iff the
replay never attempted a disabled firing, i.e.\ the trace stays within $\mathcal P$ at
every step; and $1-\Fitness$ is a normalized $\ell^1$ measure of excursion outside the
enabled set --- a distance to the polytope boundary.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lawful trace has unit fitness; the converse localizes the fault]
A trace is a firing sequence of the net iff $\Fitness=1$ under token replay. If
$\Fitness<1$, the first frame at which a token is consumed on an unenabled transition
is a coordinate-localized witness $t^\star$ of the violation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Token replay fires each event by removing pre-tokens and adding post-tokens; a firing
is \emph{enabled} iff $\bm m\ge\bm m^-_t$. If every event is enabled, no forced
consumption occurs, numerator $=0$, $\Fitness=1$, and by induction the sequence is a
genuine firing sequence, so the marking stays in $\mathcal P$. Conversely if some event
is not enabled, replay records a forced consumption at the earliest such index; that
index is $t^\star$, the numerator is positive, and $\Fitness<1$. \end{proof}

\section{Separability: decomposing the geometry}

The polytope of a realistic process is astronomically high dimensional, but the
processes that occur in practice are \emph{separable}: recursively built from
sub-processes each of which is locally either pure choice (a state machine) or pure
concurrency (a marked graph).

\begin{theorem}[Separable decomposition, after Kourani--van der Aalst]
\label{thm:sep}
A sound workflow net that is separable admits a recursive decomposition into a
POWL~2.0 model in which each node is either a choice graph (all exclusive/cyclic logic)
or a partial order (all concurrent logic). Each node's local marking geometry has
dimension bounded by the node's arity, independent of the global net size.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Separability is a Rice quarantine for processes]
Theorem~\ref{thm:sep} has an admission reading. \emph{Separable} nets are exactly those
whose geometry decomposes into bounded lanes; the decomposition algorithm terminates and
certifies membership. Non-separable nets are \emph{refused} with a stated reason. Thus
separability is the admission predicate of the process layer: it is the decidable
sub-language onto which arbitrary workflow logic is retracted, precisely as $\Adm$
retracts $\Obs$ in Chapter~\ref{ch:obs}. The high-dimensional interior is real; each
lane is bounded; the boundary artifact (the decomposition certificate) is small.
\end{remark}

\chapter{The Instance: A Machine No One Held in His Head}
\label{ch:instance}

\section{Setting}

We instantiate the theory on a concrete autonomous build. Over a bounded interval a
fleet of automated agents performed exploration, planning, and implementation across a
constellation of code repositories. The interior of this process consumed on the order
of
\[
\Sigma_{\text{tokens}} \;\approx\; 2.5\times 10^{6}
\]
machine-generated tokens --- surveys, plans, diffs, tool transcripts --- \emph{no
human read in full}. Let $\dim\Sigma$ denote the effective dimension of this interior;
it is, for our purposes, unbounded relative to $\CL$.

\section{The Comprehension--Verification Gap}

\begin{corollary}[Comprehension--Verification Gap]\label{cor:gap}
Let $\mathrm{Comp}(\sigma)$ be the cost of comprehending the interior trace and
$\mathrm{Ver}(\sigma)$ the cost of verifying its boundary projection. Under
Corollary~\ref{cor:noncomp} and Theorem~\ref{thm:faithful},
\[
\mathrm{Ver}(\sigma) \;=\; O(\CL)\ \text{per receipt},
\qquad
\mathrm{Comp}(\sigma) \;=\; \Omega(\dim\Sigma),
\]
so the ratio $\mathrm{Comp}/\mathrm{Ver}$ grows without bound in $\dim\Sigma$ while
verification stays constant. Trust is available at cost $O(\CL)$ for a system of
unbounded interior.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Verification reads only $\Proj(\sigma)$, of size $\le\CL$, and performs a fixed hash
recomputation and comparison per checked frame (Theorem~\ref{thm:faithful},
Corollary~\ref{cor:noncomp}); hence $O(\CL)$ per receipt. Comprehension requires
ingesting the interior, whose size is $\Theta(\dim\Sigma)$; hence
$\Omega(\dim\Sigma)$. The ratio therefore diverges. \end{proof}

\section{Instantiation of the boundary}

Concretely, the entire $2.5$-million-token interior projects to a handful of
$\CL$-sized coordinates of the form
\[
\bigl(\text{verdict}=\textsf{pass},\;
h_+ = \texttt{<256-bit hex>},\;
\Fitness = 1.0,\;
\text{refusals} = k \text{ (each receipted)}\bigr),
\]
together with a coverage figure $\text{pass\_rate}\in[0,1]$ over an enumerated frontier
and a differential-oracle agreement flag. Each is at most a few chunks; each is checkable
without reading the interior; and by Theorems~\ref{thm:faithful} and
\ref{thm:conservation} each is faithful to the interior it summarizes. The refusals are
not omissions but recorded cells (Chapter~\ref{ch:obs}, Proposition~\ref{prop:semilattice}):
a refusal register is the join-support of the denial monoid, materialized.

\section{The antibody clause, formalized}

\begin{proposition}[Self-audited scale]
Let a system emit claims $c$ and receipts $r(c)$. Define the \emph{overclaim set} as
claims lacking a faithful receipt: $\mathrm{Over} = \{c : \nexists\, r(c)\ \text{with}\
V(\Proj)=1\}$. If the system enforces $\mathrm{Over}=\varnothing$ as an invariant (the
$\textsf{docs-exceed-mechanism}$ defect class), then the admissible magnitude of a claim
is bounded solely by the mechanism that receipts its limits, not by the claim's rhetoric.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By construction every admissible claim has a faithful receipt, verifiable at cost
$O(\CL)$ by Corollary~\ref{cor:gap}; a claim without such a receipt is in $\mathrm{Over}$
and rejected by the invariant. Hence the set of admissible claims is exactly the set of
receipted claims, whose magnitude is bounded by what the mechanism can receipt, not by
what the narrative asserts. \end{proof}

This is the formal content of the doctrine: \emph{a grand system without receipts is
grandiosity; a grand system that receipts its own overclaims is machinery.}

\chapter{Conclusion}

We set out to justify, rather than assume, the trust engineers already place in systems
they cannot comprehend. The justification is a projection: admission retracts undecidable
observation onto a decidable sub-language (Rice quarantine, Chapter~\ref{ch:obs}); a
deterministic bounded morphism manufactures artifacts from admitted observations (the
Chatman equation, Chapter~\ref{ch:mu}); and a collision-resistant chained receipt
projects the unbounded execution trace onto a coordinate of size $\le\CL$ that a human
can verify without reading the interior (the Faithful Projection Theorem). The calculus
of the attention fluent explains scheduling and the infinite price of unlawfulness
(Chapter~\ref{ch:calc}); the geometry of the marking polytope makes conformance a
membership question with separating-hyperplane certificates and localizes faults
(Chapter~\ref{ch:geo}); and separability is the process-layer admission predicate that
keeps each lane bounded while the whole exceeds any head. The instance
(Chapter~\ref{ch:instance}) exhibits the theory operating on a $2.5$-million-token
interior whose trustworthiness rests entirely on boundary receipts.

The standard was never comprehension. The standard was a faithful projection, small
enough to hold and guaranteed by the mechanism. Beyond cognitive load; within admissible
projection.
