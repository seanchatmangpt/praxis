# Bibliography: Swarm Algebra Foundations

This bibliography documents the foundational publications and original papers underpinning the Swarm Algebra concepts, including symmetric monoidal categories, sheaf theory, Čech cohomology, and homological algebra.

---

## 1. Symmetric Monoidal Categories

### Saunders Mac Lane (1963)
*   **Title:** Natural associativity and commutativity
*   **Author:** Saunders Mac Lane
*   **Year:** 1963
*   **Publication Venue:** *Rice University Studies*, Vol. 49, No. 4, pp. 28–46
*   **DOI:** None (Reprint DOI: [10.1007/978-1-4615-7831-4_19](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4615-7831-4_19))
*   **URL:** [http://hdl.handle.net/1911/62865](http://hdl.handle.net/1911/62865)
*   **BibTeX:**
    ```bibtex
    @article{maclane1963natural,
      author  = {Mac Lane, Saunders},
      title   = {Natural associativity and commutativity},
      journal = {Rice University Studies},
      volume  = {49},
      number  = {4},
      pages   = {28--46},
      year    = {1963}
    }
    ```
*   **Swarm Relevance:** This foundational paper introduced monoidal categories (originally "categories with multiplication"), symmetric monoidal categories, and the coherence theorems (pentagon and hexagon relations) that establish the unique isomorphism between parenthesized tensor products.

## 3. Čech Cohomology and Sheaves in Cohomology

### Eduard Čech (1932) — Original Paper
*   **Title:** Théorie générale de l'homologie dans un espace quelconque
*   **Author:** Eduard Čech
*   **Year:** 1932
*   **Publication Venue:** *Fundamenta Mathematicae*, Vol. 19, pp. 149–183
*   **DOI:** None
*   **URL:** [http://eudml.org/doc/212569](http://eudml.org/doc/212569) (Alternative DML-CZ URL: [http://dml.cz/dmlcz/501004](http://dml.cz/dmlcz/501004))
*   **BibTeX:**
    ```bibtex
    @article{cech1932homologie,
      author  = {{\v{C}}ech, Eduard},
      title   = {Th{\^e}orie g{\^e}n{\^e}rale de l'homologie dans un espace quelconque},
      journal = {Fundamenta Mathematicae},
      volume  = {19},
      pages   = {149--183},
      year    = {1932},
      url     = {http://eudml.org/doc/212569}

    ```
*   **Swarm Relevance:** Introduces the nerve of an open cover and uses it to define homology groups for general topological spaces via inverse limits. This forms the basis of Čech homology, which was subsequently dualized to Čech cohomology.
