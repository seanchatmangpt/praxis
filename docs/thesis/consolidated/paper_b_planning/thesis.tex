\begin{abstract}
This paper consolidates two prior theses in this series --
\emph{Mission Geometry: Planning, Conformance, and Separability for
Bounded Receipted Execution} and \emph{The Projection Principle} -- into
one account, organized around a single keystone: the manufacturing
morphism $\Act = \muop(\Adm)$, its chained receipt, and the Comprehension--
Verification cost-regime gap it makes possible, with PDDL planning,
Petri-net conformance, POWL separability, and lexicographic cost
arbitration stated as that keystone's concrete geometric instantiation.
Every claim is marked evidence-bound or speculative: evidence-bound
claims cite the exact corpus RDF label and Lean~4 kernel receipt (bare
Lean~4 core and, separately, a Mathlib-linked lane) that grounds them;
speculative claims -- a market-framed revenue-separation theorem, three
planetary-scale engineering estimates, an interpretive claim about
refusal-vocabulary legibility, and a self-audited-scale antibody clause --
are quarantined into a single dedicated chapter, never mixed silently
into the technical chapters. Three of the keystone's own central
theorems -- Faithful Projection, the noncomprehension corollary, and
Conservation of Consequence -- are reported as stated but not
machine-verified in either Lean lane on the receipts currently on disk,
a limitation stated plainly rather than smoothed over.
\end{abstract}
