\begin{abstract}
This paper consolidates two sources in the Chatman Equation thesis
series: \emph{04\_projection\_and\_scale}, which instantiates the
keystone's Comprehension--Verification Gap theorem at fleet scale and
derives a decidable-oracle discipline, a branchless byte-level
admission construction, and a receipting invariant bounding claim
magnitude; and \emph{synthesis\_thesis}, which derives -- rather than
hand-authors -- the supervision topology and failure geometry that keep
a fleet of agents running under fault, and reports the resulting
10,000-member cell's measured fault-injection numbers. Every paragraph
below carries a machine-checkable evidentiary register,
\textsc{evidence-bound} or \textsc{speculative}; the planetary-scale
economics chapter and one flagged proposition in the Fable-harness
chapter are the only speculative material, and neither is mixed
silently with the evidence-bound chapters around it.
\end{abstract}
