\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The first edition of this paper showed a plan that had been hand-authored
becoming a \emph{derived} artifact: from declared capabilities and a
saturated fact base, a bounded solver discovers order and bindings, executes
the result as a content-addressed DAG, and admits it through refinements
that recompute rather than trust. The second edition traced every layer's
scientific supply chain. Both editions shared a hidden assumption with
almost all planning literature: \textbf{every step succeeds}. This edition
removes it. Erlang's answer to failure --- isolate, supervise, restart ---
was designed for a world where the crash space could not be seen in
advance; the instruments of modern physics (the LHC's triggers, the EHT's
reconstruction) teach the opposite discipline: predict the event
signatures, then measure. We fuse the two: the \emph{planner's own
analysis} now derives, before runtime, a supervision topology (stages and
restart strategies earned from the plan's data-dependency structure) and a
failure geometry (per-node ordered branches: named class, signal
conjunction, lawful response), both content-addressed into the plan's
receipt lineage. At runtime, crashes are values; every crash lands in a
named branch or in the implicit, unshadowable \textsf{GeometryGap}; the
four lawful responses (restart within an 8-bounded intensity, park with a
guaranteed way back, refuse with a certificate naming the culprit fact,
escalate) actuate under receipt; surrender itself is an \emph{Ok} result.
The machinery is measured, not asserted: at 10{,}000 supervised cell
members, supervision overhead is statistical noise, throughput is flat
across fault rates from 0\% to 50\%, recovery counts track injected rates
exactly, and a crash-looping workload class is quarantined by a
cross-group quorum within one epoch --- while an unmodified foreign
verifier still accepts every receipt. The design is a distillation of a
real lineage (CNS's 8T/8H/8M doctrine, BitActor's tick budgets, ByteActor's
bounded rings, knhk's classification-without-actuation), ported with
receipted provenance and zero new dependencies. The thesis, extended:
\textbf{authorship placed correctness in a head; derivation places it in a
chain --- and a chain that names its own failure modes before they occur
is the only kind a trillion agents can share.}
\end{abstract}

\section{The Removed Assumption}

A derived plan is a proof object: the solver's search establishes that the
step sequence reaches the goal \emph{if each step behaves}. The first two
editions stopped there, as does most of the planning literature --- PDDL
semantics, like Hoare triples, speak of what holds \emph{after} an action,
never of the action dying halfway. Real fleets die halfway constantly. The
question this edition answers:

\begin{quote}
\emph{Can failure handling be derived from the same declarations the plan
was derived from --- so that recovery, like the plan, is a proof object
rather than an improvisation?}
\end{quote}

Two traditions supply half-answers each.

\begin{lineage}[Armstrong: failure as a normal, supervised event]
Erlang/OTP \cite{armstrong} made three moves that survive every
re-examination: processes are isolated so failure cannot spread by memory;
supervisors --- not the failing code --- own the recovery decision; and
restart strategies (\texttt{one\_for\_one}, \texttt{rest\_for\_one},
\texttt{one\_for\_all}) plus a \emph{restart intensity} (max $N$ restarts
per window, then give up upward) turn ``let it crash'' into a bounded,
structured discipline. What OTP does \emph{not} do is derive the tree: a
human writes the supervision hierarchy, and the crash space --- what can
fail, with what signature, deserving what response --- lives in the
programmer's head. Armstrong designed for a world where the far side of
the moon could not be seen.
\end{lineage}

\begin{lineage}[The instrument sciences: predict, then measure]
The LHC does not record every collision; its trigger system encodes, in
advance, the signatures of events worth keeping \cite{lhc}. The EHT did
not photograph a black hole; it reconstructed one from lawful projections
gathered by an instrument \emph{designed around a predicted signal}
\cite{eht}. The discipline common to both: enumerate the event geometry
before the event, instrument for exactly those signatures, and treat
anything outside the geometry as a first-class finding --- never as noise
to be discarded silently.
\end{lineage}

\begin{lineage}[The 8-bounded actor lineage: CNS $\to$ BitActor $\to$ ByteActor $\to$ knhk]
A parallel, in-house lineage spent 2025--2026 building the execution-side
half. CNS fixed the doctrine: \textbf{8T/8H/8M} --- at most 8 ticks per
hot operation, 8 hops per workflow, 8-byte quantization --- and shipped
the canonical BitActor engine (\texttt{BITACTOR\_TICK\_BUDGET 8},
zero-tick fast path, BLAKE3-sealed spec$\leftrightarrow$exec)
\cite{bitactor}. ByteActor V3 productized it: 64-byte crystal envelopes,
per-core bounded SPSC rings (the explicit anti-Akka move: no unbounded
mailboxes, no GC pauses), ``Erlang coordinates, C executes''
\cite{byteactor}. knhk carried the doctrine into ${\sim}250$K lines of
Rust: a branchless \texttt{TickBudget} with the Chatman constant, a
compile-time \texttt{ChatmanBounded} assertion, an R1/W1/C1 runtime-class
taxonomy with per-class failure policies, and a \texttt{ParkManager} that
demotes over-budget work with a typed cause \cite{knhk}. The lineage's
three invariants never wavered: declared semantics compiled down;
8-bounded execution with a zero-cost fast path; hash-sealed provenance.
And the lineage's one persistent gap never closed: \textbf{classification
without actuation}. knhk can say \emph{what kind} of failure occurred and
\emph{what should} happen; nothing in the constellation restarts,
re-admits, or closes the loop. Its parked work has no way back; its MAPE-K
loop re-plans overlays but restarts nothing.
\end{lineage}

This edition is the fusion: OTP's actuation vocabulary, the instrument
sciences' predict-then-measure stance, the lineage's bounded machinery ---
and the synthesis substrate's own novelty, \emph{derivation}, applied to
all of it. (Dependency note, receipted: the knhk machinery is
\emph{ported}, not linked --- live build probes showed even its leanest
crate drags an async/telemetry stack, and its receipt algebra is
SHA3/ed25519 against praxis's BLAKE3 trust root. Every port carries a
greppable \texttt{PORT(knhk)} provenance tag; the crate gained zero new
dependencies.)

\section{Derived Supervision Topology}

\begin{definition}[Dependency depth and stages]
Let $\Dag = (V, E)$ be the plan's data-dependency DAG (edge $u \to v$ iff
an add-effect of $u$ feeds a precondition of $v$; first edition, \S4).
Define $d(v) = 0$ for sources and $d(v) = 1 + \max_{u \to v} d(u)$
otherwise. A \emph{stage} $S_k = \{v : d(v) = k\}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Earned strategy]
Stage $S_k$ supervises \textsc{RestForOne} iff some $v \in S_k$ has a
nonempty transitive dependent set $D(v)$; otherwise \textsc{OneForOne}.
The \emph{cohort} of $v$ is $\{v\} \cup D(v)$ under \textsc{RestForOne}
and $\{v\}$ under \textsc{OneForOne}.
\end{definition}

The strategy is not chosen; it is \emph{earned} by position: a producer
whose outputs feed later work restarts together with that work, because
its failure invalidates them; a leaf restarts alone. Two OTP strategies
are deliberately absent, and the absences are load-bearing:

\begin{refusal}[No \textsc{OneForAll}]
OTP justifies one-for-all by shared mutable fate between siblings. In an
acyclic data-flow plan, same-depth siblings are independent \emph{by
construction} --- the coupling one-for-all encodes is inexpressible. The
variant does not appear in the \texttt{Strategy} enum at all; an
exhaustive-match test makes its future addition a compilation event,
forcing the doctrine conversation.
\end{refusal}

\begin{refusal}[No \textsc{SimpleOneForOne}; intensity $> 8$ refused, not clamped]
Derived plans have static step sets (dynamic children arrive via the
incremental-assertion path, edition one \S2.4). And
\texttt{RestartPolicy::new(9, \_)} returns a \textsf{Refusal} --- the byte
governor applies to recovery itself, and silently clamping a stated policy
would be the executor editing the operator's law.
\end{refusal}

\begin{proposition}[The topology is a value]
$\Topo = (\mathrm{stages}, \mathrm{policy})$ is produced only by
\textsf{derive} (a sealed constructor: no literal construction compiles),
and $\mathrm{topology\_hash} = \ca(\mathrm{stages} \,\Vert\,
\mathrm{policy} \,\Vert\, \mathrm{plan\_hash} \,\Vert\,
\mathrm{problem\_hash})$ joins the receipt lineage. Determinism is
test-pinned: same plan, same hash.
\end{proposition}

On the five-step lawobject chain the derivation yields five singleton
stages, the first four \textsc{RestForOne} (each feeds everything after
it) and the leaf \textsc{OneForOne}; the root's cohort is the whole plan,
the leaf's is itself --- exactly the tree an Erlang engineer would have
written by hand, now emitted by the same machinery that emitted the plan.

\section{Derived Failure Geometry}

\subsection{Classes, signals, responses}

\begin{definition}[The eight classes]
$\mathsf{FailureClass} = \{$\textsf{LogicFault}, \textsf{BudgetBreach},
\textsf{AuthorityVacuum}, \textsf{TransientFault}, \textsf{Stall},
\textsf{StarvedInput}, \textsf{CertifiedUnsat},
\textsf{GeometryGap}$\}$ --- the semantic axis, capped at eight by the
doctrine. knhk's R1/W1/C1 tiers ride orthogonally as data on each crash
snapshot; fusing the axes would yield 24 variants, which is how
taxonomies die.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Branch, geometry]
A \emph{branch} is $(c, \Sigma, r)$: a class, a conjunction of signal
predicates over a crash snapshot (retries-at-least, ticks-above-budget,
crash-kind, refusal-head, no-progress, upstream-parked), and a lawful
response $r \in \{\textsf{Restart}, \textsf{Park}(\rho),
\textsf{Refuse}(\mathrm{core}), \textsf{Escalate}\}$. The \emph{geometry}
$\Geo$ maps each node to an ordered branch list; classification is
first-match-wins; $\mathrm{geometry\_hash} = \ca(\Geo \,\Vert\,
\mathrm{topology\_hash})$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The gap is unshadowable]
\label{thm:gap}
\textsf{GeometryGap} is the value classification returns when no stored
branch matches; it is never a member of any stored list (a structural
invariant, test-enforced over every derived geometry). Consequently no
branch ordering, addition, or deletion can capture, mask, or remove the
gap outcome: the map admits its own incompleteness \emph{by construction},
not by convention.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Immediate: an unstorable value cannot be shadowed by stored values, and
first-match-wins terminates in the implicit case exactly when all stored
matches fail. The enforcement is the derivation function's type: branch
lists are built from a constructor set that excludes the gap class.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Mining the branches from the plan's own analysis}

The geometry is not a template; parts of it are \emph{computed from the
problem}. The decisive case:

\begin{definition}[Fragile fact]
A precondition predicate $p$ of capability $c$ is \emph{fragile} iff
\textbf{no} capability in the problem produces $p$. The initial state is a
one-time gift: if a fragile fact is lost mid-run, nothing in the plan can
lawfully re-produce it --- whereas a fact with even one producer is
recoverable by restarting that producer.
\end{definition}

For every fragile fact, derivation emits an \textsf{AuthorityVacuum}
branch responding $\textsf{Refuse}([\textsf{MissingFact}(p)])$: the same
producer analysis that powers Solver8's pre-search unsat certificates
(edition one, \S3), now aimed at runtime loss. In the self-application
domain, \texttt{push}'s \texttt{authorization} precondition is fragile;
the derived geometry therefore guards, \emph{before runtime}, exactly the
gate the release doctrine cares about --- and a runtime revocation of
authorization refuses in \textbf{one crash, zero futile retries}, with the
fact named in the certificate (test-pinned).

An instructive honesty note: the first implementation of fragility
($\neg\mathrm{init} \wedge \mathrm{producers} \le 1$) was wrong in both
directions --- it guarded recoverable facts and missed init-only ones ---
and was caught by its own two tests failing with \emph{inverted}
expectations. The corrected rule ($\mathrm{producers} = 0$) is the one
proved above. The method continues to catch its author.

The remaining branches encode doctrine as order: a tick breach
(\textsf{TicksAboveBudget}) refuses \emph{before} the time-tier
over-budget branch can park it --- the hot path never retries over budget
(knhk's R1 discipline, upgraded from a metrics counter to a certificate);
\textsf{Stall} (second attempt, no progress) outranks
\textsf{TransientFault}; a bad output earns exactly one restart and then a
\textsf{Manual} park, because \emph{retry does not heal wrong
computation}.

\section{Supervised Execution: the Loop Closed}

\subsection{Crashes are values}

Under \texttt{forbid(unsafe\_code)} there are no panics to catch. The
executor's runner trait returns
$\mathrm{Result}\langle(\mathrm{bytes},\mathrm{ticks}),
\mathsf{NodeCrash}\rangle$, with a blanket implementation over every
infallible runner --- existing code is untouched, and a crash is data:
\textsf{Io}, \textsf{BadOutput}, \textsf{OverBudget},
\textsf{PreconditionLost}, \textsf{Refused}. This is the lineage's
crystal-envelope instinct (state that travels as a small, total value)
applied to failure itself.

\subsection{Actuation semantics}

On crash: snapshot $\to$ classify $\to$ named branch $\to$ actuate, every
step receipted into a chain seeded
$\fold(\HB(\texttt{geometry/v1}),\ \mathrm{geometry\_hash})$ --- so the
chain's genesis \emph{is} the derived map, and replaying it proves
recovery followed the geometry rather than improvisation.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \textbf{Restart}: retry within intensity; upstream work replays free
through the memo/WAL layer (edition one's kill\,-9 durability composes
here unchanged --- process death mid-restart-loop recovers to the same
receipt).
\item \textbf{Park}: a \textsf{ParkedEntry} with a derived re-admission
policy --- \textsf{OnInputChange} (something upstream was fixed),
\textsf{AfterRuns}$(n)$, or \textsf{Manual} (only authority re-admits) ---
closing knhk's no-way-back gap; parked entries persist through the same
BLAKE3 WAL, so \emph{quarantine survives machine death}. Dependents are
skipped with attribution (\textsf{SkippedBy}), never silently.
\item \textbf{Refuse / Escalate}: the run halts with the branch's
certificate inside the receipt; the crash chain is never lost to the
halt.
\item \textbf{Surrender}: intensity exhaustion yields
$\mathsf{RunOutcome{::}GaveUp}$ --- an \emph{Ok}, not an \emph{Err}.
Lawful surrender is a result with standing; only lawless states are
errors.
\item \textbf{The gap}: an unmatched crash emits a \textsf{GeometryGap}
receipt, takes the safe default (restart), and the run \emph{completes}
--- with $\mathrm{geometry\_conformance} = \mathrm{false}$ reported
honestly. A gap is a finding, not a failure.
\end{itemize}

\begin{proposition}[Total accounting]
Every node of every supervised run carries exactly one disposition
(\textsf{Completed}$(r)$, \textsf{Parked}, \textsf{SkippedBy},
\textsf{GaveUp}); no silent rows exist (test-pinned:
$|\mathrm{dispositions}| = |V|$).
\end{proposition}

\begin{measurement}[Recovery is invisible in the artifact]
On the lawobject plan with two injected transient crashes at \texttt{judge}:
both crashes land in named branches (\textsf{TransientFault}, then
\textsf{Stall} --- the second identical crash correctly classified as
no-progress), the run completes, and the final root hash is
\textbf{byte-identical} to the crash-free run's. Park-then-re-admit heals
to the same identity: run 0 parks the over-budget node and skips its
dependents; run 1's re-admission pass completes the plan with upstream
served from memo, root again byte-equal to crash-free. Machine death
composes: kill\,-9 mid-restart-loop, WAL recovery, identical receipt.
\end{measurement}

\section{The Supervised Cell: Composition Under Failure}

Supervision must not break the cell's composition law (the cell hash is a
function of group roll-up roots alone; edition one's minimum
phase-change cell). Three design rules keep it intact, each test-enforced:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \textbf{Additive-only receipts.} New counters
(\textsf{recovered}, \textsf{parked}, \textsf{geometry\_gaps},
\textsf{restarts}, the quarantine set, the epoch-plan chain) are
serde-defaulted fields \emph{outside} all hashed material. The invariant
$\mathrm{admitted} + \mathrm{refused} = n$ holds (parks live inside
refused; recoveries inside admitted), the fold functions are
byte-identical --- and the \textbf{unmodified} Python foreign verifier
accepts every supervised receipt (tested).
\item \textbf{Combo status lanes.} All eight status-byte bits are taken,
so supervision claims provably-unreachable \emph{combinations}:
$\textsf{S\_RECOVERED} = H|A$ (the base run never co-sets halted with
admitted --- proven by enumeration), $\textsf{S\_PARKED} = H|B$,
$\textsf{S\_GEOMETRY\_GAP} = H|U|E$. agent8's SWAR sweep is compatible by
construction: a recovered member still carries $A$ and still counts as
admitted at 9.76\,G agents/s with zero code change.
\item \textbf{Determinism through faults.} Injection is a seed-keyed
splitmix64 lottery; a member's byte, restarts, and terminal hash replay
exactly given the seed (pinned over 24 agents $\times$ 2 runs), so
selective challenge survives supervision.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{MAPE-K, closed at epoch boundaries}

knhk's MAPE-K loop analyzed and never actuated. Here it closes, with one
discipline: \emph{parameters change only at epoch boundaries} ---
mid-epoch mutation is refused by construction (the quarantine set is
immutable within an epoch), preserving per-epoch replay. The roll-ups
\emph{are} the monitor plane; analysis detects a crash-looping template by
\textbf{cross-group quorum} (the same template parking in $\ge 3$ distinct
groups in one epoch --- replication across independent shards is the
evidence bar that the template, not the host, is at fault); the plan is a
receipted \textsf{EpochPlan} whose hash chains under its own domain; the
execute step is the next epoch's quarantine set, whose refusals surface in
the \emph{existing} refusal register through the \emph{existing}
bucketing --- zero register changes.

\begin{measurement}[The supervised cell, 10{,}000 members, release]
\label{meas:cell}
Overhead of supervision at fault rate $0$: $-0.9\%$ \emph{of medians over
five paired runs}, inside the baseline's own $\pm 5.7\%$ run spread ---
statistical noise; the healthy path is untaxed. (The first edition of this
measurement reported $-2.7\%$ from a \emph{single} sample; the harness now
carries a self-refutation guard --- a negative overhead exceeding the
baseline spread \emph{fails} the run instead of being published --- and
the single-sample figure is retired.) Per-member latency at $10\%$ faults:
p50 $34\,\mu$s, p99 $1.42$\,ms, worst $2.29$\,ms --- the tail the
aggregate throughput was masking; verdicts are percentiles and worst
cases, never minimums. Composition ratio (whole cell over the sum of
individually timed members): $1.0004$ --- the roll-up machinery's priced
cost. Recovery counts track injected transient rates exactly:
$69 / 608 / 3{,}179$ recovered at $1\% / 10\% / 50\%$. Throughput is
\textbf{flat} across all fault rates (${\sim}15{,}000$ members/s,
recomputed from count and elapsed time). The crash-looping template is
detected at quorum and quarantined by epoch 2 in every configuration;
final-epoch members of that class refuse cheaply instead of burning
restarts. Every cell verifies in Rust, and \texttt{foreign\_verify.py}
--- byte-unmodified --- verifies the supervised receipts. (Receipt:
\texttt{receipts/supervised\_cell.json}; regenerable by the committed
harness.)
\end{measurement}

\begin{remark}[What flat throughput means]
The cost of a supervised fleet tracked its \emph{failure novelty}, not its
failure \emph{rate}: recovered transients are absorbed at boundary cost,
and the one genuinely novel pathology (the crash-looping class) was
converted --- once, by quorum --- into a cheap standing refusal. This is
the overlap-curve law of edition one (\emph{deliberation cost is linear in
novelty, invariant in fleet size}) reappearing on the failure side, which
is precisely what the trillion-agent thesis predicts supervision must do.
The full novelty-curve-under-faults re-measurement is receipted as
deferred (Refusal~\ref{ref:curve}), not claimed.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The Rust Fable Testbed: Spec-Driven LLM Evaluation}

The \code{rust-fable-testbed} crate applies the synthesis substrate's oracle discipline to
the evaluation of LLM-generated code. Instead of trusting that a model's output is correct,
every proposed code block is subjected to a multi-stage compilation and test oracle before
being admitted into the plan's receipt lineage.

\begin{definition}[Fable harness and mock model membrane]\label{def:fable}
A \emph{Fable harness} is a test environment that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*)]
\item constructs a \emph{prompt} $P_T$ from a task ontology $T$ (a typed description of
      the coding task, its inputs, outputs, and invariants),
\item calls a \emph{mock model membrane} $M$ that intercepts the LLM API, returning a
      deterministic response drawn from a test fixture indexed by $P_T$'s hash,
\item extracts the code block $C$ from the model response $R$, and
\item runs the verification oracle $\mathrm{Oracle}(T,R)$ (Definition~\ref{def:sandbox})
      against $C$ in an isolated \code{cargo} workspace.
\end{enumerate}
The mock membrane ensures that harness runs are hermetic: no real LLM call is made, the
response is deterministic given $P_T$, and the oracle verdict is reproducible.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[The Fable oracle is a decidable oracle in the sense of Def.~4.1 of
Paper~04]\label{prop:fable-oracle}
$\mathrm{Oracle}(T,R)$ as defined in Definition~\ref{def:sandbox} is a decidable oracle
for the correctness question ``does $C$ implement $T$ correctly?'' on the finite test
corpus generated by the harness: it is total, terminating, and labelling (pass or fail
with a structured error from the failing stage).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Each stage of the oracle (\code{cargo build}, \code{cargo test}, \code{cargo clippy},
\code{safety\_audit}) terminates on every finite $C$ (the Rust compiler and test runner
always terminate on finite input with no infinite loops in the toolchain). The codomain
of each stage is $\{0,1\}\times\mathrm{ErrInfo}$, a total finite type. A failure at any
stage short-circuits with the stage index and its \code{ErrInfo}, analogous to the
staged validator of Paper~04's Definition~4.7. Hence $\mathrm{Oracle}$ is total and
terminating, and by Proposition~4.4 of Paper~04 any observed failure is a genuine
defect in $C$, not a property of the oracle.
\end{proof}

\begin{construction}[Receipt chaining for LLM code artifacts]\label{con:fablechain}
When $\mathrm{Oracle}(T,R)=1$, the harness mints a receipt using the standard
\code{OcelCausalFrame} chain:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fablechain}
h_{+}\;=\;\chainH\bigl(h_{-}\,\Vert\,\mathrm{body}(\fr)\bigr),
\end{equation}
where the frame's \code{obj\_refs} carry $\dg(C)=\chainH(\text{canonical bytes of }C)$
(Construction~\ref{con:merklecell} for the group-level context and Definition~\ref{def:walframe}
for WAL durability). The genesis seed is $\chainH(\text{``fable-v1-genesis''})$, domain-
isolated from the plan chain so fable receipts cannot cross-verify with plan receipts even
if their terminal hashes collide.
\end{construction}

\begin{remark}[Mock membranes as differential oracles]
The mock membrane produces responses from test fixtures; a real-model run produces a
different response. Running both and comparing (differential oracle,
Proposition~4.2 of Paper~04) bounds the false-accept rate: an implementation error that
passes the oracle with the mock response but fails with the real response is caught by the
differential. This is the \code{rust-fable-testbed}'s production validation path: mock
fixtures provide determinism; occasional real-model probes bound the divergence.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Spec-Driven Sandbox Verification Oracles}

LLM evaluation harnesses evaluate code correctness using mock model membranes and sandboxed execution environments.

\begin{definition}[Sandbox Verification Oracle]\label{def:sandbox}
Let $P_T$ be a prompt compiled from task ontology $T$, and let $C$ be the code block extracted from model response $R$. The sandbox verification oracle is the composite:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sandbox}
\text{Oracle}(T, R)\;=\;\text{cargo\_build}(C) \ \land\ \text{cargo\_test}(C) \ \land\ \text{cargo\_clippy}(C) \ \land\ \text{safety\_audit}(C).
\end{equation}
The outcome is receipted and chained to the ledger using rolling BLAKE3 hashes.
\end{definition}

\section{Hierarchical Merkle Cell Rollups and Write-Ahead Logs}

To scale verification, agents are grouped into hierarchical Merkle rollups.

\begin{construction}[Hierarchical Merkle Cell Rollups]\label{con:merklecell}
Let $M_{g,i}$ be the terminal hash of member $i$ in group $g$. The Group Receipt hash is:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:groupreceipt}
H_g \;=\; \chainH\Bigl(\,\text{sort}\bigl(\{ M_{g,i} \}\bigr)\,\Bigr),
\end{equation}
and the Cell Receipt hash is the rolling fold over $G$ group hashes:
\[
H_{\text{cell}}\;=\;\chainH\bigl(H_1\ \Vert\ H_2\ \Vert\ \dots\ \Vert\ H_G\bigr).
\]
\end{construction}

\begin{definition}[Write-Ahead Log Frame]\label{def:walframe}
A cache frame journaling the memoized DAG nodes is serialized as a length-prefixed frame:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:walframe}
\text{WAL\_Frame}\;=\;\langle\,\text{length},\ \chainH(\text{payload}),\ \text{payload}\,\rangle,
\end{equation}
allowing recovery to discard torn frames.
\end{definition}

\section{The Refusal Register}

\begin{refusal}[No path dependencies on knhk]
Verified by live probes, recorded as the standing reason: the leanest
candidate crate drags tokio/reqwest/opentelemetry transitively; the
$\mu$-kernel ships property-testing libraries as regular dependencies and
uses unsafe transmutes; the workspace does not build as a whole. Ports of
50--200-line designs, tagged \texttt{PORT(knhk)} with per-item deltas,
cost less than the supply chain. Drift is greppable.
\end{refusal}

\begin{refusal}[No measured CPU ticks]
knhk's own hot receipts carried dummy tick values behind a hardcoded
4\,GHz assumption. Declared deterministic costs (\textsf{Ticks} as an
abstract unit) are the honest model for a planner; rdtsc instrumentation
is a bench follow-up, documented as a divergence, not smuggled in as a
claim.
\end{refusal}

\begin{refusal}[No novelty-curve-under-faults measurement --- yet]
\label{ref:curve}
Member-level fault injection recovers without re-running solver work, so a
work-proxy re-measurement would \emph{overstate} the cache dividend ---
the flattering error. The claim is withheld until node-level injection is
wired through the fleet path; the receipt file names this in its own
deferred list.
\end{refusal}

\begin{refusal}[v1 scope: beat scheduling, full R1/W1/C1 SLO matrix, quarantine parole, supra-cell supervision]
Each receipted with salvage: the enum and one tier transition ship; epoch
boundaries already provide the re-admission cadence beats would refine;
quarantined classes await a parole mechanism; cells supervising cells is
the composition step after this one.
\end{refusal}

\section{Conclusion: What Derivation Now Covers}

Edition one: the \emph{plan} is derived --- order and bindings discovered,
not authored. Edition two: every derivation stands on a named supply
chain. This edition: the \emph{crash space} is derived --- the supervision
tree earned from the plan's own dependency structure, the failure branches
mined from the solver's own fragility analysis, both hashed into the same
receipt lineage as the plan, so that at runtime every failure either lands
in a branch that existed before the failure did, or surfaces as an honest,
unshadowable gap.

Armstrong made failure survivable when the crash space was unknowable.
The instruments made hidden events reconstructable by predicting their
signatures. The lineage made execution bounded and sealed. The synthesis
substrate adds the step none of them had: \emph{the machine that derives
the work now derives the failure of the work} --- and measures, rather
than asserts, that supervision costs nothing on the healthy path, tracks
novelty rather than rate under faults, and converts recurring pathology
into standing refusals by quorum.

\begin{center}
\emph{Let it crash} was the right answer when the crash space was
unknowable.\\
\textbf{Derive the crash space} is the answer when the planner can see it
first.\\[0.5em]
A trillion agents do not need a trillion failure handlers.\\
They need one law: every crash lands in a named branch, or names a gap ---
and either way, there is a receipt.
\end{center}
