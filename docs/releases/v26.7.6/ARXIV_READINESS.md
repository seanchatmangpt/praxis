# arXiv Readiness — Praxis v26.7.6 "After Neon"

Proposed title: **Praxis v26.7.6 After Neon: Verified AI Engineering
Infrastructure for Manufacturing Standing in AI-Generated Technical Work**

Status of this document: readiness assessment, not a submission. Every claim
cites a file, test, receipt, or commit in this repository. Rows without
evidence are marked accordingly.

## 1. Abstract (draft)

AI systems now produce code, plans, and mathematical statements faster than
human review can grant them standing. Praxis is an infrastructure that
manufactures standing instead of asserting it: artifacts enter through a
law-state graph (closed vocabularies, unknown predicates refused by name),
are searched by a PDDL planner, structured as POWL workflows, executed
through bcinr hot paths, generated by a deterministic factory (`ggen`), gauged
by the Lean 4 kernel and by tests, and evidenced by computed BLAKE3
genesis-folded receipts with no wall clock in any hash path. We report one
end-to-end empirical result: a 219-statement mathematics corpus (202
distinct labels) routed through automated Lean 4 kernel verification,
yielding a disjoint receipted partition — 179 verified, 4 rejected, 18
dependency-blocked, 17 deduplicated in the standalone lane
(`docs/thesis/math_manufacturing/rdf/thesis.ttl`), and 178 of 202 labels
subsequently verified against a Mathlib-linked Lake toolchain (commit
`1ea2385`, per-statement receipts in
`tools/paper-factory/lean-lake/mathlib_migration_receipts.jsonl`). We frame
this as a second instantiation of the manufacturing morphism `Act = μ(Adm)`
already implemented once as an RDF-to-PDDL planning compiler, and we state
what the system does not do without euphemism.

## 5. Methodologies to describe

1. **Receipt methodology** — BLAKE3 genesis-folded chains, `ts_ns=0`, typed
   `Refusal` on any deviation (`src/lib.rs` Refusal enum; invariants 1–3).
2. **Verification methodology** — Lean 4 kernel as admission gate via
   `crates/praxis-lean` (commit `09dcda2`), with reject-fix-retry waves and
   real kernel errors fed back (commit `1ea2385` message; 12 waves, 184
   invocations).
3. **Formalization methodology** — search-Mathlib-before-axiom discipline
   established in the 4-file pilot (commit `dab70b7`), applied corpus-wide.
4. **GraphLaw methodology** — N3/Datalog materialization, SHACL/ShEx gates,
   closed vocabularies with refusal-by-name
   (`docs/releases/v26.7.6/GRAPHLAW_FEATURES.md`; `crates/praxis-graphlaw`).
5. **Planner methodology** — PDDL search over graph facts (pddl-index
   dictionary-encoded lazy grounding; `docs/releases/v26.7.6/PLANNER_SURFACE.md`).
6. **bcinr–POWL–PDDL methodology** — plan → POWL workflow decomposition
   (`crates/powl2-decompose`, Kourani Stage-1 WF-net→POWL) → bcinr hot-path
   execution (bcinr-powl-receipt/bcinr-pddl/bcinr-powl deps, root
   `Cargo.toml:96-100`) → ggen factory emission.

## 6. Limitations (must appear in the paper)

- 24/202 labels not Mathlib-verified; `thm:faithful` excluded outright
  (crypto/probabilistic content with no Mathlib formalization — commit
  `1ea2385` message).
- Some Lean files declare axioms where Mathlib lacked the type/theorem; the
  verified count measures kernel acceptance, not axiom-freeness. The paper
  must report the axiom count per file (derive from
  `tools/paper-factory/lean-lake/Praxis/Corpus/*.lean` before submission).
- Single-operator, single-machine result; no external replication.
- The economic chapter is speculative by construction and must stay flagged.
- `praxis-reconciler` is orphaned from the workspace (INVENTORY.md) — nothing
  in the paper may depend on it.
