# Working Backwards Press Release

## Praxis Announces Blue River Dam: Deterministic Control Infrastructure for AI-Generated Enterprise Work

Pasadena, CA — Praxis today announced Praxis v30.1.1: Blue River Dam, the first
enterprise control plane designed to govern AI-generated technical work from
proposal to standing.

As AI systems generate code, proofs, plans, specifications, policies, reports,
tests, compliance artifacts, and operational workflows at unprecedented speed,
enterprises face a new bottleneck. The problem is no longer whether AI can produce
work. The problem is whether institutions can control, admit, verify, audit, and
accept that work without drowning in it.

Praxis v30.1.1 solves this problem by applying deterministic flow control to
AI-generated work.

The release combines GraphLaw, PDDL planning, POWL workflow execution, bcinr
branchless transition kernels, ggen artifact manufacturing, verifier gates, receipt
chains, Divan control benchmarks, and publication reports into one closed
enterprise system.

The result is Blue River Dam: a deterministic control structure for generated
technical flow.

AI creates the water. Praxis controls the river.

## The Enterprise Problem

Enterprises are adopting AI agents faster than they can govern the work those
agents create.

Agent frameworks can generate tasks, call tools, route workflows, produce traces,
and retry failures. But most cannot answer the basic production questions that
determine whether generated work can be safely admitted into an enterprise system:

- How much generated work is in progress?
- Where is it waiting?
- How long does it take to receive standing?
- Which verifier is the bottleneck?
- Which artifacts are blocked?
- Which claims are speculative?
- Which actions are legal next?
- Which receipts prove the last transition?
- What happens when generation doubles?

These are not optional questions. They are factory questions. If an AI framework
cannot answer Little's Law questions, it cannot control AI-generated work.

## The Blue River Dam Doctrine

A system that is slower than the flow can only observe. A system that is faster
than the flow can govern.

AI agents operate in probabilistic language time — milliseconds to minutes. Praxis
operates the control layer in deterministic system time: state transitions,
workflow ticks, standing updates, action eligibility checks, receipt links, and
branchless control paths benchmarked in nanoseconds, microseconds, and bounded
local execution windows.

Praxis is not another agent framework. Praxis is the dam.

# Vision v30.1.1

## The world before Blue River Dam

By 2030, enterprises no longer suffer from a shortage of generated work. They
suffer from generated overflow. Every product team has AI agents; every engineer
has code assistants; every compliance team receives AI-drafted evidence; every
research group uses autoformalization; every security team sees machine-generated
findings; every executive asks for AI-generated plans.

The result is not clarity. It is pressure. Generated artifacts pile up faster than
humans can admit them. Workflows fill. Queues grow. Compliance lags. Verification
stalls. Reports drift. Managers see dashboards but not standing. Developers see
traces but not acceptance. Executives see activity but not control.

This is the failure mode of probabilistic enterprise automation: a city of pumps
with no dam.

## The world after Blue River Dam

AI can generate as much as it wants. But generated work does not enter production
directly. It enters Praxis. Praxis admits it, shapes it, plans it, routes it,
checks it, receipts it, measures it, publishes it.

Every generated artifact has a flow lane. Every flow lane has WIP. Every WIP pool
has a cycle time. Every cycle time has a bottleneck. Every bottleneck has a planned
next action. Every action has preconditions. Every transition has effects. Every
effect has receipts. Every report has standing.

## The strategic thesis

The future enterprise will not be won by the company with the most agents. It will
be won by the company with the best acceptance infrastructure. The bottleneck is
not generation. The bottleneck is standing.

Standing requires: admission, shape, law-state, planning, workflow, verification,
receipts, replay, publication, control. Praxis v30.1.1 unifies those surfaces.

## The mathematical frame

Little's Law: L = λW. If arrival rate (λ) increases and time to standing (W) does
not fall, work in progress (L) explodes. AI increases λ. Praxis reduces W.
Therefore Praxis controls L.

PDDL gives the grammar of lawful next action. POWL gives the grammar of executable
workflow. bcinr gives the hot-path control substrate. GraphLaw gives the current
state of standing. ggen gives artifact manufacturing. Lean, Lake, tests, and
validators give gauges. Receipts give replayable evidence. Divan gives benchmarked
proof that control surfaces are fast enough to govern.
