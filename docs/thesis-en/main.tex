\begin{abstract}
This dissertation constitutes a fundamental paradigm shift in the history of computational intelligence, formally inaugurating the Industrial Revolution of Knowledge. By abandoning heuristic approximations in favor of strict combinatorial maximalism, this work lays the mathematical bedrock for autonomous recursive workflows. Across ten mathematically exhaustive chapters, we establish the definitive algebraic, geometric, and calculus structures that govern execution dynamics. This includes the S1-S6 verification pipelines formalized via discrete Morse theory, computational decidability algebra, exact sequence cohomology, and Martin-Löf dependent type theory. The theoretical scaffolding presented herein unequivocally resolves the automation verification asymmetry, providing the absolute verifiable framework for Artificial General Intelligence.
\end{abstract}
