\chapter{Combinatorial Maximalism in Distributed Systems}

Combinatorial Maximalism, while not a universally standardized term, intersects with well-established fields such as Combinatorial Topology for Distributed Computing and the study of "Maximal" problems like Maximal Independent Set (MIS) or Maximal Matching (MM). Combinatorial topology provides a framework using simplicial complexes to determine the solvability of distributed tasks under failure models. Meanwhile, maximal problems address fundamental symmetry-breaking challenges essential for distributed algorithms.
