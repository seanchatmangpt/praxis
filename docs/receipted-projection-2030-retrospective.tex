\begin{abstract}
\noindent
In 2026 we proved a projection engine correct by exhaustion --- $144$ write
cells, $\binom{8}{2}$ pack pairs, delta-group laws --- and stated a
conjecture we did not yet dare call a theorem: that under context-bounded
agentic construction, \emph{authority computed at every layer and claimed at
none} would become the default trust posture of industrial software. This
retrospective closes the loop. Between 2026 and 2030 the stack --- the
\textsf{ggen} projection engine, the pack distribution calculus, chained
decision receipts, triple admission gating (2028), and cross-organisational
receipt federation (2029) --- was adopted across regulated Fortune~500
environments in banking, pharmaceutical manufacturing, telecommunications,
aerospace, and insurance. We restate the original mathematics in its mature
form: the write discipline as an exhausted product space whose boundary
provably carried the defect distribution; pack composition as a simplicial
complex that grew from $8$ vertices to registry scale ($10^{4}$ packs)
without abandoning the pairwise-generation theorem, because the two
interaction channels were held closed by governance; federation as a functor
from the supply digraph to the category of receipt chains, whose limit
condition \emph{is} the audit; and the intent-residue ratio as a measured,
monotone trajectory rather than a slogan. We report what broke, what the
mathematics predicted about where it would break, and the one deployment
truth no theorem anticipated: the hardest migration was never technical ---
it was persuading institutions that a verifier which refuses is more
valuable than a narrator which reassures. By 2030, an unreceipted change in
a governed estate is treated as an unsigned binary was treated in 2020:
not wrong, merely \emph{unadmittable}. That sentence, unremarkable to
today's reader, is the result.
\end{abstract}

\chapter{What We Claimed in 2026, and What Happened}

\section{The claim}

The Chatman Equation, in its live form
\[
  F_{n+1} \;=\; \mymu\bigl(O^{*}, C^{*}, P^{*}, T^{*}, F_{n}\bigr),
  \qquad
  R_{n+1} \;=\; \mathrm{receipt}\bigl(F_{n+1} - F_{n}\bigr),
\]
asserted that a codebase is not an authored artifact but the lawful
projection of an admitted graph into a live filesystem, with every write
decision bound into a tamper-evident chain. In 2026 this held for one
engine, eight packs, and $182$ tests. The 2027 conjecture was distributional
(every open-source project ships a pack); the 2030 conjecture was
epistemic (receipts displace review as the unit of trust).

\section{The adoption curve, compressed}

\begin{casestudy}[Banking, 2027--2028]\label{dep:bank}
A global systemically important bank adopted pack projection for its
regulatory-reporting integration surfaces. The driver was not velocity but
\emph{attestation cost}: quarterly evidence packages that had consumed
audit-team months collapsed to chain verifications executed in minutes.
The bank's regulators accepted receipt-chain heads as primary evidence in
2028 --- the first time in our records that a supervisor consumed
$\rec$-values directly. The decisive property was not that the code was
correct but that \emph{drift was refused by name}: FM-coded failures gave
examiners a closed vocabulary of everything that could have gone wrong.
\end{casestudy}

\begin{casestudy}[Pharmaceutical manufacturing, 2028]\label{dep:pharma}
Computer-system validation (CSV) under GxP had always been the industry's
context-loss pathology in institutional form: documentation asserting what
systems do, drifting silently from what they did. Projection inverted the
burden. Validation protocols became SHACL feasible-region definitions;
the installed system became $\mymu(O^{*})$; and the validation evidence
became the receipt chain itself. Re-validation after change --- historically
weeks --- became replay: $O_n = O_0 \oplus \bigoplus_i \del_i$, verified
term-by-term.
\end{casestudy}

\begin{casestudy}[Telecommunications, 2028--2029]\label{dep:telco}
A tier-one carrier projected its network-service catalogue (thousands of
service variants) from a governed ontology. This was the first deployment
to hit the boundary of the pairwise-generation theorem
(Chapter~3): service packs began feeding \texttt{construct} entailments
into one another's \texttt{select}s, breaking the two-channel factorisation.
The response was the theorem's own contrapositive: the interaction was
\emph{re-closed} by introducing declared entailment interfaces, restoring
pairwise coverage rather than abandoning exhaustion.
\end{casestudy}

\begin{casestudy}[Aerospace and insurance, 2029--2030]
Aerospace brought the strictest reading: receipts co-signed by deterministic
old-AI verifiers only, LLM output permanently classified
$\mathrm{LlmProjection}$ and excluded from every evidence path --- the
2026 doctrine (``Old AI is the factory; LLMs are the brochure'') adopted
verbatim as a certification requirement. Insurance closed the loop from the
other side: actuarial models began \emph{pricing} unreceipted software
estates as a distinct (and expensive) risk class, which did more for
adoption in 2030 than any engineering argument had done in 2027.
\end{casestudy}

\section{The honest ledger}

Adoption was neither smooth nor universal. Three frictions recurred:
(i) the \emph{handlers negotiation} --- every organisation initially
over-estimated its intent residue, claiming hand-written status for code
that was in fact projectable; the measured ratio (Chapter~5) settled the
argument each time; (ii) \emph{ontology politics} --- once triples were
gated, asserting one became an act with an owner, and institutions
discovered they had never actually decided who owns their nouns; and
(iii) the \emph{refusal shock} --- teams accustomed to warnings experienced
fail-closed as hostility for roughly one quarter, until the first prevented
incident converted them. None of these were engineering problems. All of
them were the engineering surfacing decisions that had always been made
implicitly, in nobody's context.

\chapter{Algebra, Matured}

\section{The exhausted core, unchanged}

The write-discipline product space
$\Sig = F \times U \times I \times S \times T$, $|\Sig| = 144$, has not
grown a dimension since 2026 --- by policy. Every proposed frontmatter
extension since has been rejected or expressed as composition of existing
dimensions, because the governance rule adopted in 2027 states:
\emph{a new dimension multiplies the exhausted space; the proposer pays for
the new matrix}. Four years of Fortune~500 pressure produced exactly zero
additions. The lesson generalised: closure survives contact with the
enterprise precisely when the cost of opening is charged to the opener.

\begin{theorem}[Stability of the coverage theorem]
The 2026 total-coverage result
$\forall \sigma \in \Sig : \wdec(\sigma) = \hat\wdec(\sigma)$ has been
re-executed on every release for four years across all deployment estates.
No cell has ever regressed in production code. The two incidents recorded
against the write discipline (2027, 2029) both occurred in \emph{vendor
forks that had disabled the matrix} --- the boundary theorem
(Chapter~5 of the 2026 thesis) holding in its contrapositive form.
\end{theorem}

\section{Admission as a subcategory: the 2028 governance algebra}

The open frontier of 2026 --- nothing gates who may assert a triple ---
closed in 2028 with triple admission.

\begin{definition}[Admitted ontology category]
Let $\Ont_V$ be graphs over closed vocabulary $V$. An \emph{admission
policy} is a pair $(\Feas, \adm)$ where $\Feas \subseteq \Ont_V$ is the
SHACL-definable feasible region and
$\adm : \del \times \mathrm{Evidence} \rightharpoonup \Feas$ is a partial
map admitting a delta only with evidence. The admitted graphs with
evidence-carrying deltas as morphisms form a subcategory
$\Ont^{*} \hookrightarrow \Ont_V$; $\mymu$ is defined on $\Ont^{*}$ only.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Asserted/derived factorisation]
Every predicate in $V$ is classified asserted-only or derived-only, and
derived predicates factor through $\mathrm{CONSTRUCT}$ from evidence:
there is no morphism in $\Ont^{*}$ that introduces a derived triple
directly. The 2026 wasm4pm convention (\texttt{breedStatus} is never
asserted) is the special case that seeded the general law. In banking and
pharma estates this factorisation is now the control that auditors test
first, because it is the algebraic statement of ``status is earned.''
\end{proposition}

\begin{finding}[The 2028 red-team result]
When triple gating shipped, an internal red team attempted the attack the
2026 thesis had predicted: corrupt the source of truth rather than the
projection. Every attempted assertion of a derived predicate refused by
name; the successful attacks (two, both against \emph{asserted} predicates
with weak evidence schemas) forced the evidence-schema hardening of 2028Q4.
The defect distribution again lay exactly on the boundary of the exhausted
region --- this time the region of \emph{gated predicates}.
\end{finding}

\section{Federation as a functor: the 2029 supply calculus}

\begin{definition}[Supply digraph and the chain functor]
Let $G = (\mathcal{N}, E)$ be the supply digraph: nodes are producing
estates (a pack registry entry, an internal platform team, a vendor), edges
are consumption. Federation assigns to each node its receipt-chain monoid
and to each edge the \emph{head-binding} morphism: consumer receipts include
$\head(\rec_{\mathrm{producer}})$ as bound content. This assignment is a
functor $\rec_{(-)} : G \to \Rcpt$; transitive attestation is its
composition law.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Audit as a limit condition]\label{thm:limit}
An estate's supply position is \emph{coherent} iff the diagram of bound
heads over its in-neighbourhood commutes: every path from a root producer
to the consumer yields the same bound head at each intermediate node.
Verification is a traversal of $G$ recomputing $O(|E|)$ hashes ---
the property that replaced supplier questionnaires in the 2029--2030 audit
cycles of Deployments~\ref{dep:bank} and \ref{dep:pharma}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{finding}[The content-hash gap, exploited once, then closed]
The 2026 thesis predicted that binding pack \emph{content} hashes without
\emph{chain heads} permits a producer to regenerate a pack from a corrupted
upstream ontology and ship a valid content hash. In 2028 exactly this
occurred in the wild (a compromised vendor build system; contents valid,
provenance destroyed). It was the last such incident: head-binding became
mandatory in the 2029 federation protocol, and the attack class is now
structurally excluded rather than probabilistically deterred.
\end{finding}

\chapter{Geometry, Matured}

\section{The pack complex at registry scale}

The 2026 complex had $8$ vertices, $28$ verified edges, one top face.
The 2030 public registry holds order $10^{4}$ packs; full pairwise
verification is no longer executed globally, and does not need to be:

\begin{theorem}[Sharded pairwise sufficiency]
Pack interaction still factors through the two 2026 channels ---
namespace union and output-path union --- \emph{because governance holds
them closed}: registry admission requires a declared namespace (globally
unique by construction) and a declared output-path prefix (collision-checked
at registration). Under these two closures, coherence of an arbitrary
consumer set $S$ reduces to (i) per-pack vertex proofs, carried in each
pack's own receipt chain, and (ii) the consumer's local top-face sync,
which exercises the union directly. Pairwise edges are verified lazily,
per-consumer, only for the pairs that consumer actually admits --- restoring
$O(|S|^2)$ local work with $|S|$ typically under twenty.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
The telecommunications deployment (\ref{dep:telco}) is the exception that
proved the theorem's hypotheses matter: cross-pack entailment broke the
two-channel factorisation, and the repair was not cleverer verification but
\emph{re-closure} --- declared entailment interfaces that turned an open
interaction channel back into a checkable dimension. Four years of scale
have not falsified the 2026 remark that where interactions cease to be
pairwise-generated, the maximalist must widen the exhausted skeleton;
they have shown that widening is usually cheaper as governance than as
combinatorics.
\end{remark}

\section{Drift in production: the metric earned its keep}

The drift metric $d(O, O') = |O \,\triangle\, O'|$ and its artifact-side
analogue became operational quantities: estates alarm on drift radius, not
on incident reports. The doctor triad (lock drift, staleness, orphanhood)
runs continuously; in the pharma estate its time series is itself a
regulated record. The geometric fact that mattered most in practice is the
simplest: refusal keeps the state \emph{on} the fibre of the admitted fixed
point, so the distance an auditor must reason about is always either zero
or a named, receipted exception --- never an unbounded accumulation.

\begin{proposition}[Fixed points under organisational load]
The retraction property $\sync \circ \sync = \sync$ survived every scale
regime because it was enforced at two grades: state idempotence (bytes) and
trace quiescence (decisions, mtimes, events). The 2026 \texttt{force}
finding --- state-fixed but trace-live, hence oscillation under observers ---
recurred in three enterprise integrations (each coupling projection to a
change-data-capture or backup observer) and was diagnosed in minutes each
time, because the failure mode had a name, a theorem, and a matrix cell.
\end{proposition}

\chapter{Calculus, Matured}

\section{Replay as the institutional memory}

The discrete fundamental theorem
$O_n = O_0 \oplus \bigoplus_{i=1}^{n} \del_i$ stopped being a test
assertion and became the mechanism of institutional memory. In four years
of governed operation, no estate in our records has performed an
archaeology-style investigation (``what was true in March?'') by reading
code or interviewing engineers: the answer is integration of the delta
stream to the queried instant, verified against the chain. The 2026
observation that the session's own context compaction was survivable
because authority lived outside the window scaled without modification to
organisations, where the ``window'' is staff turnover and the ``compaction''
is a reorg.

\section{Sensitivity and the economics of change}

The chain rule across $\mymu_1 \dots \mymu_5$ matured into the
change-costing function enterprises actually use: a proposed
$\del O$ is priced by the bound
$\del A \subseteq \mymu_5' \cdots \mymu_1'(\del O)$ \emph{before} it is
admitted --- blast radius as a derivative, computed from the graph, not
estimated in a meeting. The banking estate's change-approval board was
reduced to reviewing exactly the deltas whose computed sensitivity crossed
declared thresholds; everything below threshold is admitted by evidence
alone. This, more than any other single mechanism, is where the review-hour
savings of Chapter~1 came from.

\section{The intent-residue trajectory}

\begin{definition}[Residue ratio]
For an estate at time $t$, let $\rho(t)$ be hand-authored bytes (the
handlers layer and gated assertions) over total admitted bytes. The 2026
proposal made $\rho$ a first-class receipt field; since 2027 it has been
measured, not argued.
\end{definition}

Across the deployments of Chapter~1, $\rho$ fell from initial values near
$0.6$ (organisations systematically over-claiming intent) toward stable
plateaus between $0.08$ and $0.15$ --- and, importantly, \emph{stopped
falling} there. The plateau is the empirical measurement of the 2026
conjecture that some context resists compression into verifiable form.
The residue did not vanish; it \emph{clarified}. What remains hand-written
in 2030 is dense with genuine decision: the handlers, the evidence schemas,
the ontology's asserted core. The calculus reading: $\rho(t)$ behaved as a
decaying process onto a positive fixed point, and the fixed point, not
zero, is the honest asymptote of the programme.

\chapter{Theorems Revisited, Four Years On}

\begin{theorem}[Locality, extended record]
The 2026 locality theorem --- every defect lies outside some exhausted
space at introduction; none found inside one after exhaustion --- extended
unbroken through 2030 across all governed estates in our records. Each
production incident class maps to a then-open dimension: the vendor forks
that disabled the matrix (open by choice); the weak asserted-evidence
schemas of 2028 (gating dimension newly opened); the provenance-destroying
regeneration of 2028 (federation dimension not yet closed); the
observer-coupling oscillations (trace dimension known but not yet enforced
in those integrations). The theorem's practical form became a planning
instrument: the incident forecast \emph{is} the map of open dimensions.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The economic theorem, now measured]
The 2026 conjecture that receipted projection inverts the
generation/verification cost asymmetry is, as of 2030, an actuarial fact:
verification is $O(\mathrm{hash})$ per link and $O(|E|)$ per supply
position; unreceipted estates carry a distinct insurance loading; and the
market behaviour predicted from the cost inversion (unreceipted
$\Rightarrow$ unadmittable) is observable policy in every regulated estate
we surveyed. Trust migrated to the cheap-to-verify artifact for the same
reason it always has --- because checking became cheaper than believing.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[What no theorem anticipated]
The mathematics predicted where systems would fail. It did not predict the
principal human finding: that institutions initially experience a refusing
verifier as an \emph{insult} and a reassuring narrator as a
\emph{colleague}, and that this preference inverts permanently after the
first incident a refusal prevents. The adoption curve of receipted
projection is, in every estate we observed, a step function whose step is
located at that first prevented incident. Engineering determined whether
the step was possible; a saved quarter determined when it occurred.
\end{remark}

\chapter{Conclusion: The Sentence That Became Unremarkable}

In 2026, ``authority must be computed, not claimed'' was a thesis defended
by one engine, eight packs, and an exhausted matrix. In 2030 it is the
unexamined default of governed software estates: graphs admit, projections
precipitate, receipts chain, federation composes, and the residue --- the
genuinely human part --- is small, named, and priced. The combinatorial
maximalist's wager closed in the black: the spaces we closed stayed closed,
the boundaries we declared carried exactly the defects we later met, and
every widening of the exhausted region retired an incident class rather
than a test suite.

The 2026 thesis ended: \emph{the boundary of what you have exhausted is the
frontier of what you may trust.} The 2030 retrospective can finally state
its converse, which four years of enterprise operation earned:

\begin{center}
\emph{What remained trustworthy is exactly what we had exhausted ---\\
and the map of our open dimensions was, cell for cell,\\
the forecast of everything that failed.}
\end{center}
